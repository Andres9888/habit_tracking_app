## 🎯 Recommended Starter Pack

If you're new to Huberman protocols, start with these 5 essentials:

### 1. Morning Sunlight Viewing ☀️
**Why**: Sets your circadian clock for the entire day
**Protocol**: 2-10 minutes within 30-60 minutes of waking

### 2. Delay Caffeine 90 Minutes ⏰
**Why**: Allows natural adenosine clearance, prevents afternoon crash
**Protocol**: Wait 90-120 minutes after waking before first coffee

### 3. Physiological Sigh 😮‍💨
**Why**: Fastest way to reduce stress in real-time
**Protocol**: 1-3 double inhales through nose, long exhale through mouth

### 4. Evening Light Dimming 💡
**Why**: Supports natural melatonin production
**Protocol**: Dim lights 2-3 hours before sleep, avoid overhead lights

### 5. NSDR Practice 🛌
**Why**: Restores focus and improves learning/memory
**Protocol**: 10-20 minutes of non-sleep deep rest (Yoga Nidra style)
