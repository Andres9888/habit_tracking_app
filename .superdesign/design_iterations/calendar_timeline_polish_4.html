CalendarTimeline — Consistency Audit

Diagnosing what feels "off" and three approaches to fix it

Diagnosis
What's Wrong: Triple Progress Redundancy
Completion progress is shown in three places simultaneously. The user's brain has to reconcile "2 of 5" (text) + a green bar + a ring in the FAB. This creates visual noise without adding information. The micro-bar specifically competes with the FAB ring for the same visual metaphor (horizontal fill vs. circular fill).
1

Triple Progress Redundancy

ProgressText "2 of 5" + MicroProgressBar + ProgressRingFAB all show the same data. The bar and the ring are especially redundant — both are progress fills with the same color.

2

Shelf → Cards Disconnect

The shelf is a dense information panel that ends abruptly. The 16px gap + surface color change makes it feel like two separate interfaces were stitched together. There's no visual "flow" from shelf to cards.

3

Information Density Imbalance

The shelf has 6 visual elements (greeting, badge, chip, strip, dots, bar) while each card has 4 (emoji, name, streak, check). The shelf feels "heavy" relative to the cards beneath.

4

Glass Chip borderRadius: 14 ≠ 16

The glass chip uses borderRadius 14px while everything else (cards, shelf) uses 16px from borderRadius.large. Subtle but contributes to the "something's off" feeling.

Option A — Recommended
Remove Bar, Let the Shelf Breathe
Remove the micro-bar (the FAB ring already handles this). Reduce shelf density by letting the day strip be the final element. The strip's completion dots + connectors already communicate weekly progress. Match the glass chip border radius to 16px for consistency.
