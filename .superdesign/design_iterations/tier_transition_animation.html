How to read
  • Slide slowly past a threshold (e.g. 19→20, 39→40, 59→60, 79→80) — both columns snap, but the right column cross-fades.
  • Slide within a tier (e.g. 25→35) — neither column changes; the real hook only fires on tier-name change.
  • Both the round (Calendar) and square (HabitChain) cells update in lockstep — same input, same timing.
  • The glow/shadow (box-shadow) blends smoothly too; watch the cell edges as you cross into Iron (40) and Legendary (80).
