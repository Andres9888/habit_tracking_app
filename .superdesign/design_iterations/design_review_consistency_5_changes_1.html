Card Border Radius — 12px → 16px

The shared <Card> primitive uses Tailwind's rounded-xl (12px) but the theme's canonical card radius is 16px — and every other card primitive (HabitCard, TemplateCard, hero) renders at 16px. The result: a subtle, persistent shape mismatch wherever <Card> sits next to a habit card.

Why it's better

  1. Eye stops snagging. The visual system detects shape mismatch subconsciously. 12 vs 16 reads as "two kinds of thing" even when both are cards.
  2. Hierarchy clarity. "Card" should mean ONE shape. Two radii silently imply two categories — but there's only one.
  3. Cheap win. One Tailwind class change in one file. Every consumer of <Card> auto-updates.

Why it's better

  5. Design changes propagate. Designer wants empty states warmer? Update once. All 11 surfaces update together.
