BEHAVIORAL SCIENCE

Paradox of Choice (Schwartz, 2004) — More options = more paralysis. 5 curated choices vs 200+ in a catalog. Fogg's Tiny Habits — Start with 2-3 small behaviors, not a catalog. The "Pick 1-3" framing gives permission to start small.

BEHAVIORAL SCIENCE

Implementation Intentions (Gollwitzer, 1999) — "I will do X when Y" increases follow-through from ~33% to ~62%. The cue options are category-aware (morning habits get morning cues). Skippable to keep Quick Add fast. +5 seconds for 2x retention.

BEHAVIORAL SCIENCE

BEHAVIORAL SCIENCE

Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan, 2000) — Autonomy is a basic psychological need. Locking frequency removes control over the most impactful parameter. Users who set their own frequency feel ownership → higher persistence.
