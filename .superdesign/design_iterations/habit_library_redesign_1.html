1. Consolidate Guidance Sections HIGH IMPACT

BEFORE
3 guidance blocks stacked (StartHere card + 5 goal cards + Featured hero) = ~650px of scroll before any actionable habits appear. New users must scroll past all guidance to find specific habits.
AFTER
Compact horizontal pill row with 5 starter habits (directly tappable +) + "Goals..." trigger for on-demand bottom sheet. Featured hero stays. Total: ~100px. Saves ~350px of scroll depth.

2. Vertical Trending Cards HIGH IMPACT

BEFORE
Horizontal carousel showing 2 visible cards + 1 peeking. Users must discover horizontal swipe gesture. Third card (88% popular) is almost invisible at 40% opacity. Social proof content hidden behind interaction.
AFTER
3 compact inline vertical cards. All trending habits visible immediately with full metadata (name, description, frequency, popularity %, science badge, add button). Zero hidden content. 100% discoverability.

3. Elevate Category Grid MEDIUM-HIGH

BEFORE
Categories buried at position 5-6 in scroll order. Tiles show faded preview emojis (noise). 6 visible initially. Plain visual treatment makes them easy to scroll past.
AFTER
Categories moved to position 3 (right after hero). Tiles have colored left border accent, bold count text, no faded emojis. 8 visible initially. Compact height (82px vs 110px). Categories are within first 1.5 viewport heights.

4. Rebrand Premium Packs MEDIUM

BEFORE
"Starter Sets" with "Premium" badge creates paywall anxiety. Vertical stack of 3 cards takes ~300px. Plain card design with emoji cluster + text.
AFTER
"Curated Packs" with overline label (no paywall signal). Horizontal scroll with gradient cards takes ~120px. Each pack has prominent habit count chip. Saves ~180px and feels aspirational, not gated.

5. Section Header Hierarchy MEDIUM

BEFORE
Every section header uses identical 17px bold text. No visual rhythm. User's eye has no signal about which sections are primary vs secondary. Page feels like a flat list of equal-weight blocks.
AFTER
Primary sections (Categories, Trending) use 19px headers. Secondary sections (Packs) use overline label + title pattern. Standardized spacing creates visual rhythm. Users can scan section importance at a glance.

6. Compact Header LOW

BEFORE
Title + subtitle ("Start with a goal, a category, or a quick add") shown to all users. 13px subtitle adds ~20px+ of vertical space. Subtitle is instructional — redundant after first visit.
AFTER
Subtitle hidden for returning users (2+ habits). Title-only header. Small gain (~20px) but compounds with other changes and removes cognitive noise for experienced users.
