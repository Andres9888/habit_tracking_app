Digital Detox

Mental Health Daily 2 min read
About This Habit

Take a scheduled break from screens and notifications to reset your attention and reduce digital overwhelm.

🔬
Scientific Backing
Research Citation

"Ward et al. (2017) - Brain Drain: The Mere Presence of One's Own Smartphone Reduces Available Cognitive Capacity"

💡 Why This Habit Works

The mere presence of a smartphone — even when turned off — occupies limited cognitive resources, reducing working memory capacity and fluid intelligence. Scheduled screen-free periods allow your prefrontal cortex to recover attentional control, improving focus, creativity, and emotional regulation.
