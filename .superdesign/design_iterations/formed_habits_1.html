Formed Habits

Habits that reached Automatic strength (85%+) can be celebrated as formed — distinct from archive. They move to a hall-of-fame, stay trackable, and free focus on habits still building.

Product intent (from deferred plan + strength algorithm)
  • Trigger: Habit at Automatic level (85–100% strength) — user chooses to mark as formed
  • Not archive: Formed habits celebrate completion; archived habits pause/remove from active list
  • Entry points: Swipe “Form” button, habit detail CTA, or prompt banner on home
  • After forming: Optional maintenance mode (lighter tracking) or view-only in Formed collection
A — Home: ready + formed section Banner surfaces habits at Automatic. Collapsed “Formed” section keeps today’s list focused.
