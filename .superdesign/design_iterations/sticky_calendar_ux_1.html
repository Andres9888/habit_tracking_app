RESEARCH BASIS
Evidence Behind Each Change
🧠
Zeigarnik Effect — Ghost Connectors
Uncompleted tasks occupy greater mental space than completed ones. A faded connector arm signals "your chain is waiting, not broken" — creating cognitive tension resolved only by completing today's habit. The 50% opacity creates a visual "incomplete task" that drives the urge to complete.
🦉
Duolingo Research — Alert Window
Duolingo's 600+ experiments show 7-day streak milestones create a 3.6x course completion multiplier. Their Streak Freeze feature (University of Pennsylvania/UCLA research) shows flexibility increases motivation. Shifting the "at risk" warning from 8pm to 7pm gives users 5 hours of runway instead of 4 — the sweet spot between urgency and anxiety.
📊
Cognitive Load — Suppress Low Streaks
Miller's Law limits working memory to 5-9 items. Every UI element competes for attention. Showing "2-day streak" adds cognitive noise without behavioral value — users haven't built enough momentum for the streak to serve as motivation. JMIR systematic review: feedback is effective when it reaches threshold significance (88% of successful interventions).
