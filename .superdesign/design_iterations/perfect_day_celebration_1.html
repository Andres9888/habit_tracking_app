What Changes on Perfect Day

Header Greeting

"Good afternoon" → "Perfect day ✨"
Progress "3 of 5" → "All done!"
Both labels turn emerald green.

Today's Day Cell

Circle changes from dark (#2D2A26) to emerald (#10B981) with a subtle glow shadow. Completion dot turns fully green.

Habit Cards

All cards get an emerald-tinted border (rgba(16,185,129,0.25)) and soft emerald glow shadow. Toggles get glow effect.

FAB Center

Ring fills to 100% with emerald glow. Icon crossfades + → ✓. Label changes to "All done!" in emerald.

Confetti + Shimmer

Gentle confetti burst (emerald + gold particles) for 3 seconds on transition. Subtle emerald shimmer sweep continues on the homepage.

Inspire Button

The gold "Inspire" pill gets a pulsing glow animation — strategic moment to surface template discovery when users feel accomplished.

Trigger Logic: Uses the existing useCelebrationState hook in BottomActionBar. Fires when completedToday === totalHabits && totalHabits > 0. The confetti burst plays once on transition; the ambient shimmer + card glow persists while all habits remain checked. Haptic feedback (success pattern) fires on the transition moment. The celebration dismisses naturally when a habit is unchecked.
Animation Timeline
