The one-line rationale

The page today is built to persuade — six sequential blocks, each removing a reason to say no. Most users give it about five seconds. The change keeps every argument but reorders for how the screen is actually used: tell the truth about cost, prove it fast, commit small. Persuasion moves from copy into behavior — the ramping button — and the long-form case moves behind one disclosure for the minority who want it.
