One card treatment. Daily Reminder and MORE TO CUSTOMIZE share the same parchment + 1px border + subtle shadow + 18px radius. What changed (3 files, ~30 lines)

  • AdvancedOptionsSection.tsx — collapse-shell uses the same surface as the Reminder card (drop the white/card-shadow override). Expanded rows lose their individual borders and shadows; they become interior list items separated by 1px dividers.
  • AdvancedOptionRow.tsx — drop the per-row border + outer radius; add a top divider via borderTopWidth: 1 on every row except the first.
  • ToggleRow.tsx (the bell card) — bell container becomes 36×36 rounded-xl colors.surface with colors.primary[700] icon, matching the squircle pattern just shipped.
  • PresetButton.tsx — replace hardcoded hexes (#ECFDF5, #f5f5f4, #10B981, #059669) with colors.primary[100] / colors.card / colors.primary[500] / colors.primary[700]; bump radius from rounded-xl to rounded-2xl.

No new theme tokens needed. No behavior change. Pure visual unification.
