Card States

Normal

Default habit card state with user's chosen accent color.

  • • 4px left accent border
  • • Stone-800 background
  • • Unchecked circular checkbox
  • • Streak badge (violet bg)

Perfect Week

7+ consecutive days with all completions.

  • • Emerald accent color override
  • • Subtle emerald gradient tint
  • • "Perfect Week" badge
  • • 100% strength bar

Streak at Risk

Incomplete & time running out (phase window ending).

  • • Amber accent color override
  • • Pulsing amber glow
  • • "At Risk" warning badge
  • • Countdown text message

Personal Best

User just hit their longest streak ever.

  • • Trophy icon celebration
  • • Violet gradient background
  • • Confetti animation (brief)
  • • "New Record!" badge

Completed Today

Habit marked complete for current day.

  • • Filled checkbox with accent color
  • • Slight opacity reduction (0.85)
  • • Today's dot in chain is solid
  • • Satisfying check animation
