Why each change

  • RENAME
    Appearance → Look & Feel. "Appearance" is system-settings jargon. "Look & Feel" is warmer and signals it covers theme + how the calendar looks, not just colors.
  • RENAME
    Behavior → Habits. "Behavior" is vague/techy. Users think in "Habits" — it names the thing the rows actually control (sort, sound, sticky header).
  • RENAME
    Notifications → Reminders. The group only has streak reminders. "Reminders" says what it does; "Notifications" implies an OS-permissions screen.
  • GROUP
    New Data & Privacy. Pulls Archived + Export together and gives Delete account a real home instead of floating loose at the bottom — clearer + safer.
  • GROUP
    About + Support → About & Support. Two tiny one-to-two-row groups merged into one. Less fragmentation, shorter scroll.
  • KEEP
    Kept: main's stats hero. Dropped: the search bar (your call). Every row + subtitle is unchanged — main already had the copy.
