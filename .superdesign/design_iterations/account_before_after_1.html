What changed

1 removed

Stats strip + streak line

Four metrics on a settings screen. “Flawless Days 0” was the first thing read. Replaced with Member since — tenure, not performance. Also deletes a habits.getTracking query that ran on every Account open.

2 changed

Paywall gets a price and a trial

Before: a feature list with no price, no trial, no ask. After: named price, trial CTA, and the free tier stated out loud (“free plan keeps 3”) so the value is legible instead of vague.

3 changed

“Edit profile” → “Change photo”

The row only opens the photo picker. Label now matches behaviour.

4 added

Softer exit before delete

Highest-intent cancel moment in the app, currently uninterrupted. A pause option above delete catches people who want a break, not an ending.

Not fixable in the mock

The free habit cap (FREE_HABIT_LIMIT = 3) is enforced only on unarchive, never on create. That’s why the screenshot shows 14 active habits on a free account. Until convex/habits/create.ts gates, the “after” paywall is still selling something users already have.
