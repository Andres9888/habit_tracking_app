What people are starting this week
A

Search-only landing

  • Cleanest possible. Header → search → straight into transformations. Nothing competes.
  • Category + attribute chips appear only inside search/results, where filtering actually makes sense.
  • Fully commits to “choose a transformation” as the one job of the page.
  • Trade-off: Quick wins / High ROI lose their always-visible entry point (still reachable via the “Quick wins” section + search).
B

Attribute lenses only

  • Drops the 7 category chips (Morning/Sleep/Fitness…) — they duplicated the transformation cards.
  • Keeps a short row of cross-cutting lenses (All · Quick wins · High ROI · New) that add a *different* axis, not a duplicate one.
  • Preserves the momentum hooks (“Quick wins”, “High ROI”) right at the top.
  • Trade-off: still one row under the header — slightly busier than A, but far calmer than today’s 10-chip strip.
Recommendation: Go with B if “Quick wins / High ROI” are valuable discovery hooks you want visible by default — it removes the redundancy (the actual problem) while keeping the useful filters. Choose A if you want the page to feel as quiet and decisive as possible. Either way, the 7 category chips come off the landing — that’s the change that fixes the busyness.
