WHY

Why strength beats streak

Smooth, not cliff-y

A streak-driven chain toggled tiers the exact day you hit day 7, 30, 100. Strength rises a fraction each completion — so material upgrades feel earned, not automatic.

Forgiving on misses

Miss a day and streak drops to zero — but strength only decays (×0.95). A gold chain after one missed day shows as mid-gold, not reset to copper. Better mirrors reality.

One scale, no mixed signals

"Strong 💪" label + gold chain = coherent. Previously, user at streak 100 saw gold chain but label said "Growing" (because habit strength algo hadn't caught up). Now the two views agree.

Hot-start, not cold-start

New users who log a few days see strength climb past 20% quickly → chain tier upgrade (copper → chain). Feels responsive. Streak-only required 7 consecutive days before any visual reward.

Single source of truth

getMaterialTier(strengthPercent) in materialTier.ts uses thresholds that exactly mirror STRENGTH_LEVELS in strengthLevels.ts. Change one, the other follows — no drift.
