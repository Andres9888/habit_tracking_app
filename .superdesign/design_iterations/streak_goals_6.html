Why a third tab?

✓ Clean separation

Goal is a different kind of data than Calendar (history) or Strength (analytics). Deserves its own canvas.

✓ Empty state = entry point

When no goal is set, the tab itself becomes the place to set one. Discoverable without clutter.

✓ Room to breathe

Dashboard can be rich without fighting QuickStatsRow or other sections for vertical space.

✓ Scales to celebration

When goal is reached, tab becomes a celebration screen with "what's next" momentum hooks.
