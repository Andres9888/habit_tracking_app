Why this is better

  • Developer mental model: Breadcrumbs are universally understood for hierarchical navigation — Year > Month > Week is intuitive.
  • Month always visible: Whether viewing current or past weeks, the green "March" or "February" label is always prominent in the breadcrumb.
  • Multi-level navigation: Tapping "2026" shows a year view, tapping "March" shows month view, tapping "Week 1" shows week picker — each level is navigable.
  • Orientation when time-traveling: When swiping back through weeks, seeing "February / Week 3" instantly tells you where you are — no mental math needed.
  • Compact and familiar: Only 16px of height for a breadcrumb that communicates year, month, AND week position.
