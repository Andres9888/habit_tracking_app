A: Greeting-only (Recommended)
Maximum simplicity. The text "3 of 5 done" gives the fraction, the FAB ring gives the visual. No duplication, full-width greeting, ~52px horizontal space freed. The greeting can even bump up to 15px for better readability.
B: Micro Progress Bar
A 3px emerald bar adds visual momentum without duplicating the ring shape. Different form factor (linear vs circular) means it complements the FAB. Adds ~11px vertical height. Nice but potentially unnecessary given the text already says "3 of 5".
C: Streak Chip
Replaces the ring with different information — your streak, not today's progress. Header = journey context, FAB = daily progress. No data duplication at all. However, streak info already appears on individual habit cards, so it's a soft duplicate.
