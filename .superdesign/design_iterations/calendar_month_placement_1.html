Recommendation: Keep Centered
Centering wins on three axes:

1. Visual gravity. The 7-day strip is symmetric and full-width. A centered header mirrors that symmetry — the eye tracks straight down from "March" to the day rings. Left-aligning creates an L-shaped reading pattern that fights the strip's symmetry.

2. Scalability with "Today →". When viewing past weeks, the right-side "Today →" link needs a home. In the centered layout, it gets its own flex column and naturally counterbalances. In left-aligned, it's pushed to the far right, creating a tug-of-war across the full width.

3. Month transitions while swiping. When rapidly swiping through weeks, the month name ("January" → "February" → "March") snaps in place at a consistent center point. Left-aligned, these transitions happen at the left edge where they compete with the user's swipe gesture origin — the center position is more visible during navigation.
