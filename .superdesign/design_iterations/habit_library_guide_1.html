State B — the prescription (same page, no navigation)

  • Tapping a chip expands a prescription card inline: an opinionated insight line, 2 sequenced habits, one CTA.
  • The "why" line is the education — short, confident, cited.
  • Below it: the full list for that problem, one-tap addable. The prescription guides; it never locks you in.
  • This state is remembered — return visits open with your problem selected and progress shown.
