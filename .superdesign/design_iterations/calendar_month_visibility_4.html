Why this is better

  • Peripheral vision cue: Color is processed ~60ms faster than text. Users register the month change before consciously reading the label.
  • Builds month "personality": Each month develops a color association over time — green for March, purple for Feb, gold for April — creating a richer mental model.
  • Minimal footprint: The 3px band takes almost zero space while delivering a strong visual signal that's always visible.
  • Smooth transitions: When swiping between months, the band color can crossfade, making the month change feel alive and intentional.
  • Consistent with premium design: Thin accent bands are used in apps like Linear and Notion for categorical coloring — feels modern and polished.
