Recommended: Composable Header Strategy
Rather than picking one variant permanently, build a composable slot system where the header's right zone and subtitle zone can be swapped based on context and user tier:

Free users: Start with V1 Clean Greeting for the first week (onboarding simplicity). After 7 days, introduce V2 Streak Shield — they now have a streak worth protecting. Rotate V3 Micro Insight occasionally (1 free insight per day, PRO badge for more).

Premium users: Show V4 Trend Spark by default (they're paying for analytics). Swap to V8 Milestone Chase when close to an achievement (2 days away). V3 Micro Insight rotates with unlimited insights.

This turns the header into a dynamic surface that evolves with the user's journey and creates natural upgrade moments. The composable approach lets you A/B test any combination without rebuilding the layout.
