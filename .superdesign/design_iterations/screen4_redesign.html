Why the proposed direction works better
  • Single full-width visual — matches BlindPathBlock, SolutionMapPreview, TierProgression, RouteRows on other screens.
  • Bars carry the metaphor: streak ends with ✕ (resets), strength has a tiny notch (dents but holds). Visual does the work the cards' delta text was doing.
  • "Same missed day. Different consequence." caption gets absorbed into the per-row delta lines — less floating text below the visual.
  • Vertical rhythm matches the rest of the flow — no narrow-cards-with-gap awkwardness.
