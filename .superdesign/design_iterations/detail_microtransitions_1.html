Before / after mock of the plan. Before panels behave like the current build (instant hard swaps). After panels simulate the proposed Reanimated transitions. Click any element or its replay button to run the moment.

1Complete button transition — the headline fix

Before — instant flip
Color, label and icon hard-swap in one frame
After — celebratory transition
300ms color fade · check springs in · label cross-fade · 1.04 pulse + success haptic
Why: this is the dopamine moment — the single interaction the whole app exists for. An instant flip gives zero reward feedback; a sub-400ms celebratory transition makes the rep feel acknowledged, which is the behavioral hook that brings users back tomorrow.

4Consistency — one press / motion / haptic language

Before — mixed feedback
Each tappable presses differently; haptics defined ad-hoc inline
After — uniform press-scale convention
All tappables: 80ms scale-in → spring out · haptics routed through patterns.ts · one shadow token · tab content matches indicator timing (press and hold to feel it)
Why: inconsistent micro-feedback reads as cheap. Users can't name it, but a uniform press/motion/haptic vocabulary is what makes the app feel trustworthy enough to host a daily ritual — the "premium not cheap" direction.
