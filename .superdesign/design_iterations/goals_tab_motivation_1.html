From scoreboard → coach

Three new additions on the active-goal state surface the user's why, nudge them based on where they are, and let them adjust without leaving.

1
Why anchor
Surfaces the user's own `why`, `identity`, or WOOP wish — the data's already in the schema, just never shown.
2
Coach line
Single line below the ring that changes with progress %. Brackets: broken, <10%, 10–40%, 40–70%, 70–95%, ≥100%.
3
Adjust goal sheet
Change or remove the goal without navigating to Edit. Reuses existing preset-chip component.
