What is the status chip?

The status chip is the small uppercase pill in the top-right of the habit detail hero: Pending or Done. It lives next to the habit name and only answers one question: “Did I complete this today?”

Why after is better

1 · Single source of truth

“Did I do it today?” is already answered by the Complete button (label/style changes when done) and reinforced by the week strip’s today cell. A third indicator doesn’t add confidence — it adds noise.

2 · Clearer hierarchy

Top-right is premium real estate. Putting a muted “Pending” there pulls the eye away from the habit name and toward a status the user already expects at the bottom action.

3 · Less redundancy tax

The hero is already dense (ring, stats, copy, week, CTA). Removing a non-unique control is the cheapest density win — zero product loss, more breathing room for titles.
