Why this is better

  • Interactive affordance: The pill shape signals tappability — users intuitively know they can tap to open the calendar picker.
  • Month progress context: "Day 5/31" with the progress bar gives a sense of position within the month — a motivational "you're here" marker.
  • Green brand integration: The pill uses the primary forest green at low opacity, tying it into the app's color language for interactive elements.
  • Consistent with streak pill pattern: The month pill mirrors the streak pill's shape language already used in the row below, creating visual harmony.
  • Dual information density: Shows month identity AND month progress in one compact row without feeling cramped.
