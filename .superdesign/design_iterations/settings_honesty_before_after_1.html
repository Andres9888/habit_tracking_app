Privacy · Terms · What's new · v2.4.1 (45) MOVED
1 Keep the name. Show the value.
“Daily reminder” would scan faster and lie — this is still a streak-slip nudge. The row stays Streak reminders. The subtitle stops selling (“Nudge before…”) and reports state: On · 8:00 PM / Off. Time still edits in the inset.
2 Collapse completion sound
Same pattern as Sort: tone lives in the subtitle (Chime). Pills stay hidden until the row is tapped. Same three tones (Chime / Pop / Rise). No rename to “Sounds & haptics” — haptics aren’t a setting here.
3 Support: 4 rows → 2
Rate and Share are one intent. Merge into Love Chain Day? → native alert (Rate / Share / Cancel). What’s new is a changelog, not a setting — it moves to the footer with Privacy, Terms, and version. Feedback stays.
4 Premium stays. Upgrade works.
The dark banner is unchanged as a conversion surface. The actual bug: Upgrade was often unwired and no-op’d. This pass wires it to the paywall. No new gating, no leftover schema rows.
5 Explicitly not this pass
Look & Feel, the profile hero, Calendar look, Account, export placement, and any new settings from schema. Those screens were already the right size — leave them.
Net: Support 4 → 2. Sound picker off the resting scroll. Reminder row tells the truth.
Zero features lost. Rate, share, changelog, tones, and reminder time all stay reachable.
