Science basis for "days to build"

Anchored to Lally, van Jaarsveld, Potts & Wardle (2010), European Journal of Social Psychology — the canonical study on habit-formation time. They found median 66 days to reach automaticity, with a wide range of 18–254 days depending on behavior complexity.

  • Simple (single trigger, low effort: drink water, take vitamin) → ~18–30 days. Default surfaced: ~21 days.
  • Average (moderate effort, single behavior: 10 min meditation, journal, stretch) → ~60–90 days. Default surfaced: ~66 days.
  • Complex / long (multi-step, high effort: 30 min workout, study session) → ~100–250 days. Default surfaced: ~120 days.

Each template gets one of the 3 growth-type buckets at seed time. The "~X days" is the bucket's midpoint, not per-template — keeps it scannable and avoids fake precision.
