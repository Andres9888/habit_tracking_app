Why this is better

  • Solves the "which month?" problem: When numbers jump from 28 to 1, users lose orientation. The boundary line and labels eliminate confusion instantly.
  • Only appears when needed: Boundary markers only show on cross-month weeks — single-month weeks stay clean and uncluttered.
  • New month emphasis: The incoming month uses the brand green color at bold weight, while the outgoing month fades to gray — visual "forward" momentum.
  • Respects spatial mapping: Labels sit above their respective day groups, leveraging Gestalt proximity to make the association obvious.
  • Zero extra taps: Information is always visible, no interaction needed to understand the month context.
