My honest read:
  • V2 (medium) is the strongest play. Named waypoints + "next stop" framing transforms the visual we already have into something that feels like guidance, not bookkeeping. The detour state for missed days is warmer and more accurate than "pause" — the GPS is still with you, just recalculating.
  • V1 is the safe ship. Pure copy update. No new visual work. Could ship this week.
  • V3 is gorgeous but risky. Curved route SVGs feel distinctive but might age fast and break at scale (5+ habits, narrow phones). Hard to glance-read. The hand-drawn-trail feel risks looking like a kids' habit app.

What V2 unlocks that path/countdown doesn't:
  • Named waypoints become memorable. "I just passed Copper Crossing" is more vivid than "I hit copper tier."
  • "Detour" is a kinder reframe of missing a day. Pause sounds neutral; detour implies the GPS is actively rerouting you back — more agency, more support.
  • The "Habit Map" home view becomes a single ownable visual identity. Three routes side by side at different scales and statuses. Screenshot-worthy. Differentiates from every habit app that just lists habits.
