Why it matters

Save pill fills to primary[600] with CTA shadow the moment there's content. Emoji grid forced to numColumns={7} so it doesn't collapse to a vertical column on web.
Why it matters

Users abandon forms when they can't tell if the submit button works.

Create-habit is the single most-used flow in the app. A Save button that reads as disabled forces users to tap-and-hope — and some just close the modal thinking the form isn't done. This shows up as creation attempts that don't convert.

Create-to-save conversion: % of users who open the modal AND submit. The fix makes the next step unambiguous.
Time-to-first-habit: On web today, the emoji grid collapses to a single column — scrolling 60+ emojis to find 🏃 takes real time. Native iOS may not share this regression; worth verifying before shipping to both.

Three cards in the Home empty state + a matching row in Settings. Each opens the existing Workshop/WOOPSection, VisionBoardSection, YourWhySection in a modal — no new content to build.
Why it matters

Pre-commitment predicts habit survival better than the habit itself.

Gollwitzer's if-then planning research (implementation intentions) shows a 2–3× stickiness lift when users commit to a specific plan before starting. WOOP, Vision Board, and Why are the app's vehicles for that — but they're invisible today.

Day-7 retention: Users who complete a WOOP or Vision Board exercise before their first habit should return on day 7 at higher rates than control.
ROI on built work: Three components shipped to production with tests. Zero entry points = zero value. This bucket is pure activation of sunk work.

E

Semantic cleanups + revert

P2 · polish pass
Today
The bottom bar's templates icon carries an amber/brown dot pulled from colors.warning. The token is named after "warning" — the dot is trying to say "new content". Two different meanings, one color. Users learn the wrong mapping.
Also in this bucket: revert EXPO_PUBLIC_DESIGNER_REVIEW_BYPASS env var + AuthGate block. Must not ship with bypass in place — effectively disables auth.
Proposed
Templates notification dot: colors.warning → colors.primary[500]. Same semantic this dot is trying to convey ("new content") now uses the color the app already uses everywhere else for "primary / action available".
Why it matters

Every semantic mismatch is a future UX tax.

The warning color is going to show up somewhere that actually means "warning" (streak at risk, data loss, premium cap). When it does, users who were trained to read amber as "new content" will misread the warning — or dismiss it, assuming it's another notification nudge.

Warning reads correctly when it fires: future streak-at-risk / data-loss / cap messages land with attention, not tuned-out recognition.
Ship safety: The DESIGNER_REVIEW_BYPASS in AuthGate was a tool for the audit. Leaving it in a release means anyone who sets the env var gets in without auth.
Findings: 01 H3 (semantic misuse of warning color), 03 H3 (same pattern in Library featured CTA). Files: BottomActionBar/BottomActionBar.styles.ts, AuthGate.tsx, .env.local. Checklist: .context/designer-review/REVERT-CHECKLIST.md.
