Why this is better

  • "You are here" map: Like a mall directory, the mini grid shows the full month with the current week highlighted — instant spatial orientation.
  • Completion heatmap at a glance: Green dots for completed days create a visible streak pattern at the month level, motivating longer chains.
  • "Week 1 of 5" context: Knowing you're early or late in the month changes your mindset — this gives that context without a separate screen.
  • Tappable for navigation: Users could tap any week in the mini grid to jump directly there, replacing multiple swipe gestures.
  • Information-dense but compact: The 98px grid packs an entire month of data into a space smaller than 3 habit card icons.
