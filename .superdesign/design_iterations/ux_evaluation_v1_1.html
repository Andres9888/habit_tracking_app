What Works
A. CompletionRing (64px) — The enlarged ring provides an immediate visual anchor. At 64px it commands attention without overwhelming. The emerald progress stroke + warm stone track (#E5E2DE) is chromatic harmony with the design system.

B. Progress-first greeting — "Good afternoon — 3 of 5 done" exploits completion bias: users see they're 60% done and feel pulled to finish. The three-state logic (none/partial/all done) provides contextually appropriate messaging at every stage.

C. StripNav arrows — 26px circular buttons with warm stone fill make navigation visually discoverable for the first time. The disabled forward arrow at 30% opacity clearly communicates temporal boundaries. This directly addresses the "hidden navigation" problem.

E. Padding alignment — 16px (px-4) now matches habit card margins, creating a unified vertical rhythm across the entire page.
🔧
Potential Refinements
Area Observation Priority
D. Shelf bottom edge The 16px radius cutoff creates a visible boundary. Consider a 2px gradient fade or remove the radius for a more seamless transition. Low
Ring celebration timing The 1.35x pulse fires instantly on 5/5. A 200ms delay after the last tap would feel more intentional, like the UI is "recognizing" the achievement. Low
Day cell "Today" label Using "Today" instead of the weekday abbreviation is excellent UX. Consider a subtle amber dot or underline below the "Today" text to create stronger visual anchoring to the dark cell below. Low
StripNav arrow feedback The 0.85 press scale is good. Adding a subtle chevron translation (2px in arrow direction) during press would reinforce directionality. Micro
💰
Monetization Potential
The tinted shelf creates a natural "premium zone" architecture. The current free tier delivers ring + greeting + strip. Premium features can expand the shelf without restructuring:

Tier 1 (Free): Ring, greeting, 7-day strip, arrows
Tier 2 (Pro): + Weekly trend sparkline, streak calendar
Tier 3 (Premium): + AI insights, mood correlation, habit analytics

The InlineTrialBar already sits inside the shelf, creating a natural conversion point. Users who see "3 of 5 done" are in a high-engagement moment — ideal for "Unlock insights about your streak" conversion messaging.
