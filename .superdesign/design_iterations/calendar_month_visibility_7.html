Why this is better

  • Zero additional space: No new rows, pills, or headers — the watermark lives in existing space behind the day strip.
  • Subconscious registration: At 4% opacity, the text is felt more than read. Your brain processes it peripherally while focusing on the rings.
  • Dramatic on month change: When swiping from Feb to Mar, the watermark crossfades — a visually elegant transition that's hard to miss.
  • Editorial design feel: Large-scale watermark text is a technique from magazine layouts — adds sophistication to the warm minimal aesthetic.
  • Simplest implementation: Just one absolute-positioned Text component with low opacity. No new state, no new logic.
