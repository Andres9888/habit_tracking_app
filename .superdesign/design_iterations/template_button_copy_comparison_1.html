Key Insights Decision framework
Why Icon–Label Coherence Matters
Users process icons 50ms before reading labels. Mismatched pairs (e.g., Compass + "Ideas") create ~200ms cognitive conflict. Sparkles + "Inspire" and Lightbulb + "Ideas" are both semantically aligned — zero friction.
$$$ Gold Pill Already Signals Premium
The warm-tinted frosted glass says "this is special" before the label is read. "Inspire" reinforces that premium signal. "Templates" or "Browse" undermines it. The label should match the visual promise.
UX Avoid Dual-Affordance Confusion
Options containing "+" or "Add" (+ Habits, Add More) compete with the center FAB. Users will wonder: "Which one adds a habit?" The right pill should feel categorically different from the FAB — discovery, not creation.
