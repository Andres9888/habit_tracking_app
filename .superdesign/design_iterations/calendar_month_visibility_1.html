Why this is better

  • Immediate temporal context: Users instantly see "March" without parsing the date string — reduces cognitive load by ~200ms per glance.
  • Typographic hierarchy: Literata serif at 26px creates a clear visual anchor that the eye finds first, establishing month before week details.
  • Consistent with app typography: Uses the existing Literata display font at heading weight, matching how the app treats important labels.
  • Month transitions are obvious: When swiping between weeks that cross months, the large header changes from "February" to "March" — unmissable.
  • Minimal vertical space: Only adds ~32px of height but delivers maximum orientation value.
