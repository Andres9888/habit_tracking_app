"Custom" growth-icons preset

A 6th chip in the preset row that remembers YOUR emoji set. Survives switching to Fitness/Space/etc — tap Custom and your handpicked emojis come right back.

Before 5 built-in presets
🌱🌳⚡Plants
💧🏋️🏆Fitness
🌑🌕🌌Space
💭🎓✨Mind
🪵🌋👑Fire

Edit a slot to build your own set → tap Fitness → your custom emojis are gone. No way back unless you remember them.

After 6th chip: Custom
🌱🌳⚡Plants
💧🏋️🏆Fitness
🌑🌕🌌Space
💭🎓✨Mind
🪵🌋👑Fire
⭐🎯🏆Custom

Edit any slot → Custom chip auto-saves your set. Tap Fitness → Custom chip still there. Tap Custom → your exact set restored.
