Why this slot?

  • Calendar answers "which days?"
  • Strength answers "how solid is the habit?"
  • This week answers "how long total?" — the question you just asked.
  • Goal answers "how far from my target?"

First-step scope: this card only. No new tab, no schema beyond the optional minutes field on the existing tracking row, no streak/strength changes. We can iterate to a deeper time-history view (monthly / all-time totals) later.

Mock fidelity: phone uses the actual app tokens — parchment background #F5F1ED, card #FFFDFA, surface #EDEAE5, Literata for display, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for numerals. Habit accent shown as #B8742D; in the app it pulls habit.color.
