Monetization Strategy

Free Tier
Included
  • Checkmark completion icon
  • Chain link completion icon
  • Toggle between both in Settings
  • Works on HabitCard + Calendar ring
Premium Icon Packs
Part of Premium
  • Star, Flame, Diamond, Heart, Bolt, Growth icons
  • Animated completion effects per icon
  • Seasonal / limited-edition icon drops
  • Natural upsell from Settings → Icon picker

Why This Drives Monetization

Surface area: Icon picker lives in Settings → Preferences, right next to the free chain/check toggle. Users discover premium icons naturally while customizing.

Low friction: Tapping a locked icon shows the paywall. No extra navigation. The "PRO" badge on the icon pack row creates gentle FOMO.

Retention hook: Once users pick a premium icon, it becomes part of their identity. Switching away from "their" flame or diamond icon creates switching cost.

Seasonal drops: Limited-time holiday or themed icon packs (snowflake in winter, cherry blossom in spring) create urgency and re-engagement.
