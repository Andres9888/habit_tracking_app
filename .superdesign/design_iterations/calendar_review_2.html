CalendarTimeline
Visual Refinement

After removing the streak pill and value footer, the timeline card needs rebalancing. Four variants compared for visual weight, alignment, and proportion.

Component CalendarTimeline
Sprint Post-streak removal
Variants 4
Size mismatch — 36px vs 44px Calendar day circles are 22% smaller than habit chain squares, creating a visual weight gap between header and content.
Header-heavy balance 44px CompletionRing now dominates after footer removal. The ring is the same size as a chain square but towers over the 36px day cells.
Hardcoded nav colors Arrow buttons use #fafaf9 / #e7e5e4 — won't adapt to dark mode theme colors.
Empty day affordance Non-today/non-complete day circles have #fff background — they disappear into the card. A subtle fill improves tap affordance.
