Animation Analysis & Recommendations

Entrance Animation (Variation 1)

  • • 50ms stagger delay between each emoji creates flow
  • • Overshoot (1.15x) then settle adds playfulness
  • • Y translation gives sense of items "popping up"
  • • Total duration: ~450ms (fast enough to not delay interaction)

Selection Animation (Variation 6)

  • • Scale 1.15x peak → settle to 1.08x creates bounce
  • • Y-lift (-8px → -4px) creates depth/floating effect
  • • Shadow expansion reinforces lift perception
  • • Emerald shadow color maintains brand consistency

Avoid These Patterns

  • • Glow Pulse (V3) - continuous animation is distracting
  • • Wiggle (V2) - rotation feels too playful/juvenile
  • • Confetti (V4) - particles add complexity, minimal payoff

Implementation Notes

  • • Use FadeIn.delay(index * 50) for stagger
  • • Use withSpring with damping: 8-12 for bouncy feel
  • • Add translateY to scale animation for lift
  • • Respect reduceMotion by skipping entrance stagger
