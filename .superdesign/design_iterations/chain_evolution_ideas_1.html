CHAIN EVOLUTION

Leaning into the chain metaphor

Five directions, ordered lowest-lift to highest. Each reuses the existing streak tiers in strengthConfig.ts — no new data needed.

01

Material progression

The chain literally upgrades its material at streak milestones. A satisfying forge animation plays on upgrade day.

Recommendation

Start with Concept 01 (material progression) — it reuses the strengthConfig tiers already in the code and gives the chain a visible evolution story with no new data. Layer Concept 03 (charms) on top once 01 ships — purely additive visuals, high emotional payoff. Save 02 / 04 / 05 for a later pack.

Tradeoff: gamification can cannibalize the habit itself — rewards should be incidental, never block or nag the user.
