CalendarTimeline Design Review

After streak & footer removal + alignment fix — current state & improvement proposals

Design Audit Findings

visual
Size mismatch: Calendar day circles are 36px while habit chain squares are 44px (22% smaller). Creates a visual "weight gap" — the header feels lighter than the content it governs.
theme
Hardcoded nav arrows: Arrow buttons use #fafaf9/#e7e5e4 instead of theme colors. Won't adapt to dark mode.
balance
Header-heavy layout: After removing streak & footer, the header row (nav + 44px CompletionRing) visually outweighs the day cells row (36px circles). The ring is the same size as a habit chain square but much larger than a calendar day cell.
polish
Day cells lack background: Non-today/non-complete day circles have #fff background — they blend into the card and feel "empty" rather than tappable. Adding a subtle stone-50 fill creates tap affordance.
align
Alignment offset applied: pl-1 (4px) added to day row compensating for the 4px accent strip on habit cards. Centers should now align vertically between timeline and chain.
