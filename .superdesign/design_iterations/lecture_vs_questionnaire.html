How they differ where it matters

A · Lecture + 1 pick B · Full questionnaire
Total screens 7 13
Input screens 1 (one tap) 5–6 (goal, pains, tinder, categories, demo, account)
Time to first input ~25–35 s (slot 4) ~10–15 s (slot 2)
When the strength mechanic is shown Slot 3 (early) — and again personalized in 5/6/7 Slot 10 plan preview — buried after a survey
Personalization depth Deep on one axis (the user's habit drives slots 5/6/7) Wide & shallow (each answer touches one screen, then nothing)
Risk: feels like a survey Low High — the deletion in PR #1327 was a vote against this feel
Risk: under-explains the mechanic Low — lecture frames Copper / Iron / Gold by slot 3 Higher — tiers don't appear until plan preview
Conversion ceiling at paywall Moderate — less sunk cost (1 input) Higher in theory — more sunk cost, IF users finish
Drop-off risk pre-paywall Low — fewer steps High — every input screen sheds users
Day-1 retention setup Strong — habit pre-picked, no empty-state Strong — full plan pre-built
Reuse from PR #1327 work Partial — pick screen + processing reusable High — most of it already shipped once
Aligns with current branch direction Yes — extends what you're building No — undoes the deletion you just made

My read: A is the right shape for this app because the mechanic (strength tiers) is novel and needs early teaching. B is the right shape when the mechanic is familiar (meditation, calorie counting, language) and the question is which flavour the user wants. You're in the first camp. The hybrid — steal the pain-points multi-select format from B (slot 3) and the notification priming pattern (slot 11) — gives you most of B's conversion levers without the survey feel.
