Habit Card Tap Affordance

Habits home / list screen — before vs after mock. Tap the After card to feel press feedback.

Why we're changing this

On the habits list, two different tap targets do different things — but only one looks interactive today:

  • Tap the card → opens habit detail (progress, calendar, settings)
  • Tap a day dot → checks in for that day

Right now the card only shows a faint gray chevron. Most users focus on the week chain (which does look tappable) and never discover that the whole card opens detail. The UX audit flagged this: interaction affordances are effectively invisible.

Goal: make the card-to-detail path obvious without changing behavior — layered always-visible cues plus a one-time coach banner that dismisses after the first detail open.
