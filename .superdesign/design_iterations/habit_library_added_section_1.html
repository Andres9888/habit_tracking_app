How it works Added

Categories now show only what's left to add. Added habits are one tap away in a single footer, not scattered.

Why this is better

  • Browsing stays focused on what's undone. Every category only shows habits you haven't added yet, so scanning is faster and less repetitive.
  • Added habits are still reachable. They live in one collapsed "Added · N" section at the bottom — never hidden, just out of the way until you want them.
  • Category clutter drops. A category with everything already added disappears from the browse list entirely instead of showing a wall of "Added" pills.
  • Search still works everywhere. Typing filters both the active categories and the Added list together, so nothing gets lost.
