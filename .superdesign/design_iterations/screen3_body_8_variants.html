🏆 My pick after 8 variants: Variant E — Identity rungs

Why: Identity-based motivation is the strongest long-term retention lever in habit science. Reframing Copper / Iron / Gold as trying → doing → being gives the user a transformation they can map themselves onto immediately. Every screen of onboarding is selling them on what they'll become — this is the only variant that explicitly says it.

Conversion: 🟢 sharp emotional hook — "I want to be at Gold = being" is a stronger pull than "I want to know how long."
Retention: 🟢🟢 highest of the 8. Identity attachment compounds week over week — every check-in reinforces "I'm becoming a [meditator/runner/writer]." James Clear / Atomic Habits framework.
Trade-off: drops the duration explainer — "how long" gets answered later (or becomes a benefit on screen 6 instead).

Runner-up: Variant C — punchier titles + number anchors. Best for users who want concrete answers, less aspirational pull.

Quick comparison

B safest · C sharpest copy · E deepest emotional pull · F most credible · G most minimal · H most visceral · I most concrete · J easiest to scan

Each variant trades different things. E (identity) wins on long-term retention, but loses some upfront concreteness vs C or I. If you care more about conversion at THIS screen specifically, lean C. If you care about retention 30 days from now, lean E.
