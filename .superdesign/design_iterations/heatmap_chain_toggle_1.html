Heatmap chains — three visual options

Heatmap rows are days-of-week, columns are weeks. A streak runs top-to-bottom within a column then jumps to the top of the next. Same dataset in all three panels — a 32-day streak in the middle, then a few short streaks broken by missed days. Pick the treatment that lands.

A · Plain (current)
no overlay

Today's behavior. Streak readable from cell continuity alone.

B · Literal chain

Vertical bars within a column (Mon→Sun) and diagonal jumps from Sun of week N to Mon of week N+1. Most explicit; the diagonals can read busy.

C · Halo outline

Gestural — a colored outline hugs each continuous streak block. Less literal, less noisy, still says "this run is one chain."

My take: Option C (halo) reads cleanest at a glance and scales — a 60-day streak becomes one large outlined region instead of 60 bars. Option B is more explicit but the diagonals get noisy with multiple streaks. A is the baseline.
