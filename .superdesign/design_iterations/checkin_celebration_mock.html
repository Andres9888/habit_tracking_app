How to read these:

Step 14 (Check-in): the question is "what does the user actually do here?" 14A (tap one) is lowest friction and most realistic — they pick the habit they'll start with. 14B (tap all 3) is more committed but adds friction; works if you want a Cialdini-style commitment device. 14C (single intent button) is the most ceremonial — no per-habit picker, just "I'm ready."

Step 15 (Celebration): 15A is the most restrained. 15B adds a value-prop reinforcement. 15C (my pick) replaces the single cell with a 7-cell chain preview that shows what's coming next — forward-looking, narrative. 15D pushes warmth/cinematic. 15E goes wide on aspiration with a year-grid.

My recommendation: 14A + 15C. The check-in is honest and frictionless; the celebration shows the user what they're building (one link of seven for the copper tier), which makes the moment forward-looking rather than just retrospective.
