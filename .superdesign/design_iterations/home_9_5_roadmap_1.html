5 Changes to 9.5

1
Full-Width Day Strip + Header Arrows
Remove 26px button arrows from StripNav. Day cells expand to match habit card width. Compact 12px chevrons move to the date row: [<] Tue, Feb 25 [>]. Passive 10px edge indicators hint at swipe.
Navigation: 8 → 9.5
StripNav.tsx · WeekNavRow.tsx (new) · ProgressGreeting.tsx
2
Celebration → Conversion Hook
When ring hits 5/5: 200ms delay → 1.35x pulse → glow shadow. A celebration banner slides in with streak count + "See Insights →" CTA routing to premium. Fires once per day.
Premium Zone: 7 → 8.5
CompletionRing.helpers.ts · CalendarTimeline.tsx (new hook)
3
Blurred Premium Sparkline
Below day strip on free tier: a blurred sparkline with "Weekly trend" label and a "Pro" lock badge. Shows what premium users see, creating aspiration pull. Vanishes for subscribers.
Premium Zone: 8.5 → 9
CalendarTimeline.tsx · New PremiumPreview component
4
Today Amber Anchor
Add a 4px amber dot (streak.300) below the "Today" label to visually anchor the user's eye to the current day cell. Gestalt proximity principle — connects label to cell below.
Visual Hierarchy: 9 → 9.5
DayCellContent.tsx
5
Ring Track Token Alignment
Change ring track from #E5E2DE (custom) to #EDEAE5 (colors.light.card). 100% design system compliance. Also reference borderRadius.large for shelf radius instead of magic 16.
Consistency: 9 → 9.5
CompletionRing.helpers.ts · CalendarTimeline.styles.ts
