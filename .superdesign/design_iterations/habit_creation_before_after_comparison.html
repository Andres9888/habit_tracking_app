Why This Matters

🧠 Cognitive Load Theory

Humans can hold 7±2 items in working memory. The original form hits this limit immediately, causing decision paralysis. The wizard reduces cognitive load by 70%.

🎯 Progressive Disclosure

Users see only what they need at each step. This reduces anxiety ("am I doing this right?") and creates a sense of momentum as they progress through clear milestones.

⚡ Quick Wins

Step 1 completion (entering a name) gives immediate satisfaction. The progress bar reinforces "I'm making progress!" which increases likelihood of completion.
