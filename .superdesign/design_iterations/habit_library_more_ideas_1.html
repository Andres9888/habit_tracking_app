Does the prescription out-convert the popular rows? Do start-small adds retain better at day 7 than full-size adds? Today every design debate — including ours — is settled by taste.

Not a screen — a decision engine

  • ~6 events, one enum field each; piggybacks whatever analytics the app already ships.
  • Costs an afternoon; pays on every future iteration of this page.
Why: single-tier monetization = activation × retention is the whole business. We've redesigned this page three times on taste; the fourth time should be on data.
