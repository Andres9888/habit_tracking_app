Journey/Path concept — refined: countdown to habit formed

Every habit is a countdown to "habit formed". Each consistent day moves you one closer. Miss a day = pause, not setback. Strength holds, chain shows the gap, journey continues. Some are 21 days, some are 66, some are 120+ — based on per-habit difficulty (Lally 2010 range: 18–254). The user sees the whole map: where they started, where they are, where they're going. Chain + strength become coordinates on the path, not their own thing.

The model — how the pieces fit

Countdown"X days until habit forms." Set by per-habit difficulty (easy ~21d, medium ~66d, hard ~120d). From Lally 2010: 18–254 days range.
Day-by-day advanceEach consistent check-in decrements the countdown by 1. Missed days don't advance — they pause. Resume = pick up where you left off, no penalty added.
ChainThe trail you've walked — daily check-in dots laid along the path. Filled = done, dim = missed, empty = future.
Strength (0–100)How securely your foothold is at this point. Driven by recent consistency. Dips when you miss, doesn't reset.
Tier landmarksCopper / Iron / Gold appear at strength thresholds. Gold = countdown hits 0 = habit formed. Tiers gain meaning as journey waypoints.
Difficulty (1–3 dots)Per-habit. Sets the countdown length. Shown on selection so user commits with eyes open.
Habit formedWhen the countdown reaches 0, the habit is permanently "formed." Gold tier locked in. No more countdown — just maintenance. User crossed the bridge.
What this refined concept does for ChainDay:
  • Directional, not bookkeeping. "7 days until habit forms" tells the user where they're going. "Day 14 of 21" just states position. The countdown is purpose-led — it pulls forward.
  • Honest expectations from day 1. User picks "daily writing" knowing it's a 120-day countdown. No "21 days to form a habit" lie. Lally 2010 range is 18–254 — we're the only habit app honest about that.
  • Pause, not penalty, on miss. Missed day = countdown pauses. Resume = it picks up. The chain still shows the gap. Strength still dips slightly. But you don't lose ground on the destination.
  • Addresses "novelty fades" directly. The pain point from painPoints.ts ("The novelty wears off before the habit does") becomes structural: the path has length, the user knows where they are, the slog has shape.
  • Unifies what already exists. Chain + strength + tiers all stay. They become positional measurements on a journey, which is conceptually richer than three independent metrics.
  • Differentiates from streak apps cleanly. Streak counters say "X days in a row." Paths say "Day 14 of 21 on this journey, with 1 missed day along the way." More honest, more navigable.
  • Makes the in-app home visualization compelling. Three paths at three scales = a real "map of my growth." This is the kind of view people screenshot and share.

Open questions / risks:
  • What happens when a user finishes a path? Habit "graduates" → permanent? Or path extends to a longer journey (e.g., 21d → 66d for "still building")?
  • Lally 2010 ranges are real but per-individual, not per-habit category. We'd need to defensibly assign difficulties to templates. Likely defensible: research-backed for ~10 templates, "estimate" disclaimer for the rest.
  • Marker doesn't move backward on miss — but should the path get longer if you miss too many? (Probably no — that punishes, defeating the point.)
  • Visual scale problem: a 21-day path next to a 120-day path — same physical bar width but very different days/pixel. Could feel inconsistent.
