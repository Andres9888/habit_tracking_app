4 Psychological Principles at Work
😰
1. Loss Aversion
Losing a 7-day streak feels 2× worse than gaining one. The streak number creates something valuable to protect.
"I can't skip today — I'll lose my 7-day streak"
🎰
2. Variable Reward
The greeting changes based on state (Fresh start → Streak → Almost there → Perfect day). Unpredictable content triggers dopamine.
"What will the app say today? I want to see 'Perfect day'"
📈
3. Endowed Progress
Showing "3-day streak" tells users they already have progress. People are more likely to continue a journey they've started than begin a new one.
"I already have 3 days — might as well keep going"
🏆
4. Goal Gradient Effect
"Almost there!" when 4/5 habits are done accelerates completion. People speed up as they approach a goal.
"Just one more habit and I get the green checkmark"
