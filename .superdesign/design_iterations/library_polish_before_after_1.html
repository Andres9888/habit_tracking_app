The four changes

  • Real Literata serif, 26px — font now bundled and loaded at app root, so it upgrades every serif title in the app (paywall, onboarding, Make-It-Stick), not just this page.
  • Breathing room — taller hero top padding; sun removed (it was invisible anyway); subtitle stays clear of the ✕.
  • Calm white pill chips — one consistent treatment with border + subtle shadow; goal colors saved for the goal pages where they mean something.
  • Real citations per row — "Suzuki et al. (2021)" parsed from each template's reference, generic fallback only when it doesn't fit.

Why the font matters most

The mocks always rendered Literata (loaded from Google Fonts in the browser), but the app never could — the font file simply wasn't shipped. So every comparison of "app vs mock" had the app typographically handicapped from the start. With the font actually loading, the serif/sans pairing the whole design system is built on finally exists on device.
