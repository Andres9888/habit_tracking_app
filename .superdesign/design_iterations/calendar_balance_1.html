Proposed changes

Six targeted adjustments to rebalance the CalendarTimeline header-to-grid weight ratio.

Property Before After Rationale
Ring position right (isolated) left of date text Integrates the ring into the header's reading flow instead of floating alone on the right edge.
Date text alignment left (after btns) center (flex) Centering the date creates symmetry between the ring anchor on the left and nav on the right.
Nav button size 26 × 26px 30 × 30px Brings touch target closer to Apple's 44px minimum (with hit slop) and balances against the 36px ring.
Nav button layout both left split (←date→) Flanking the date text with nav buttons creates a natural < Date > pattern that users expect.
Day row padding pl-1 (4px left) px-0.5 (2px sym) Removes the asymmetric offset. Slight inset keeps cells from touching card edges.
Header backdrop none subtle bg + radius Groups all nav controls into one visual zone, separating them from the day grid below.
