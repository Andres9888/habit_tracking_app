Recommendation Summary

🥇
Option 1: Minimal
Best for your use case. Smart emoji suggestions mean most users never need to browse. Maintains your optimized fast flow.
🥈
Option 2: Subtle Label
Good compromise if you're concerned about discoverability. Small "More" label adds clarity without clutter.

Avoid Option 4: The prominent green button fights for attention with the emoji chips and encourages browsing when suggestions already work well. Option 3 is interesting but tooltips are problematic on mobile (require long-press discovery).
