Each variation is designed for a specific monitoring strength. Tags indicate the primary UX benefit. All designs use the existing app theme tokens.

4

Momentum Wave

Motivation Behavioral Science

A continuous area chart showing completion percentage across 14 days. The wave shape makes trends immediately visible — rising = building momentum, dipping = losing it.

Momentum rising 14 days

Why this works

  • Trend over snapshots — humans process slopes faster than discrete dots
  • "Don't break the climb" — a rising line creates emotional investment in continuing
  • 14-day window — long enough to see trends, short enough to feel actionable
  • Dips normalize — seeing recovery after a bad day reduces catastrophizing
