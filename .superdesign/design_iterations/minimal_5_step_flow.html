Why the 5-step middle path:
  • Welcome (1) sets warmth in 1 second.
  • Problem + Solution combined (2) shows the differentiation visually — streak counter at 0 next to a chain at strength 78. The mechanic IS the visual. Done in one screen instead of two.
  • Pick one habit (3) replaces 5 questionnaire screens — Goal, Pain Points, Category, App Demo, Pain Amp — with one direct picker. Skip allowed.
  • Account (4) moved up — gets sync set up before they're invested. OAuth-first, "Maybe later" allowed for anonymous local use.
  • Drop into app (5) with Day 1 banner explaining the action. The chain visualization sells itself once they show up tomorrow.

Conversion happens in-app, not in onboarding:
  • Trigger A: Hit 3-habit cap → upsell sheet. Echoes the "focus on three" honest-emotional voice.
  • Trigger B: Reach iron tier → gold prompt. Aspirational, after a real achievement (~Day 60).
  • Trigger C: Day 3 banner → soft trial offer. Non-blocking, dismissible, one-time.

The implementation: ~6-8 commits. Most onboarding-v2 step files get deleted; Welcome and Account survive; Plan Preview becomes the picker; new screen for combined Problem+Solution; soft paywall in-app via existing RevenueCatPaywall (just changes the trigger). The current 17-step flow in code becomes effectively unused.
