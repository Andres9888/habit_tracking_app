Text labels with underline indicator. ~32px tall. No icons, no pill container — feels editorial. Most minimal.

My pick: horizontal no-hint

Saves ~22px without losing identity — tabs still feel like "tabs" (icon + label + pill container). Hints are the right thing to cut because the hero already shows streak info and strength/goal % are one tap away. Underlined is cleaner still but feels more "web-like" and loses the iOS pill familiarity users expect in this app.
