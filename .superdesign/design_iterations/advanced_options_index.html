Advanced Options — Consolidated Redesign

Today, the Strength Algorithm picker and Growth Icons (custom progress emojis) live in two separate places inside the Habit Edit screen — mixed in with visual styling and reminder settings. The goal is to consolidate them into a single, cohesive Advanced area, positioned between behavioral settings (reminders, streak goal) and the Danger Zone. Three directions below, each with both states shown.

Variation A

Accordion with Tabs

Single collapsible card labeled Advanced. Collapsed shows preview chips with current values (Algorithm: Balanced, Growth: 🌱🌿🌳💪⚡). Expanding reveals a 2-tab segmented control (Algorithm | Growth Icons) with content swapping in place.

Best for: minimizing vertical footprint and framing the two settings as equal siblings. Tradeoff: tabs hide one control at a time — extra tap to reach the non-active tab.

Variation B

Stacked Settings Rows (iOS-style)

Under an ADVANCED section label, two full-width tappable rows — each with icon, title, and the current value in the subtitle. Tapping opens an inline sheet (algorithm cards or emoji picker).

Best for: familiar iOS Settings pattern; scales to 3+ advanced options later without redesign. Current values are always visible. Tradeoff: two sheets to build; editing requires entering a sub-view.

Variation C

Inline Disclosures (Stacked)

Under an ADVANCED section label, two independently collapsible disclosure cards stacked. Each expands in place — either or both open at once. Smallest implementation delta from today's UI.

Best for: keeping everything on one screen (no sub-sheets); users can tune both settings side-by-side. Tradeoff: tall when both open; heavier visual weight on the edit screen.
