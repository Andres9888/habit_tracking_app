CalendarTimeline — Material Tier Variants

Four takes on applying the chain's copper→legendary material tier system to the home-screen 7-day timeline strip. Each mock is interactive — scrub the slider to see the tier treatment at different growth levels.

V1 — Conservative

Streak-driven

Tier color only on completed rings. Connectors keep existing streak-based thickness. Least disruption, no new data wiring.

V2 — Balanced

★ RECOMMENDED

Average habit strength → tier. Color + connector (thickness, opacity, shimmer, glow) evolve together. Skips legendary platinum cell (awkward on round rings).

V3 — Full parity

Max drama

V2 + legendary platinum cell (gold border, pulsing halo). Exact visual parity with the chain. Biggest "you've arrived" payoff at 80%+.

V4 — Per-day

Most expressive

Each of the 7 day cells gets its own tier — the week reads as a visible growth arc. Requires per-day strength snapshots server-side.

How to compare

  1. Open each mock, scrub the slider from 0 → 100%.
  2. Watch the completed cells (Mon–Thu) shift through the 5 tier colors.
  3. Watch the connectors evolve (thickness, glow, shimmer).
  4. V2 is the recommended middle ground. V3 is more dramatic. V1 is safest. V4 is most expressive but needs backend work.
