The seven items

Change Why Where
1 Start small → detail view; description 3→2 lines Browsing is scanning, not reading. Start small is persuasion — it lands at the decision moment, not 48 times in a scroll list. Biggest single win: 2.4 → 4 cards per screen. TemplateReadRow.tsx (drop Teaser), …Header.tsx numberOfLines
2 Details pill → chevron in title row The whole card already opens details, so the pill is a signpost costing ~50px. A chevron says "tappable" for free — the standard iOS idiom. TemplateReadRowDetails.tsx folds into …Header.tsx
3 withAlpha() guard on icon tint Today `${item.iconColor}30` assumes 6-digit hex. One rgba value from the backend silently breaks the tile background. TemplateReadRowHeader.tsx + small util
5 Disable card press while importing Real race: tapping mid-import opens the preview modal while the import completes underneath it. TemplateReadRow.tsx — disabled={isImporting}
6 Compact rows in the Added group Added habits aren't shopping any more. Collapsing to icon + name + meta shortens the tail and makes the section read as history. New TemplateAddedRow.tsx, switched in CatalogSectionList
7 Popularity in the meta line popularityScore already drives sort order but is invisible — the user can't see why card #1 is first. Social proof is the strongest known nudge on template adoption. A/B this rather than ship blind. templateMeta.ts — append to label

Item 4 (no ellipsis affordance on truncated description) is dropped — the chevron in item 2 covers it. Black numbers are safe mechanical changes. Gold numbers are product calls worth measuring.
