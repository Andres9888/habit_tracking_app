Settings — before / after

Three candidate changes, rendered against the app's real warm-minimal tokens (#F5F1ED canvas, forest-green primary, stone borders). Left column is what ships today, right column is the proposal.

1  ·  Search that actually finds settings

Today search matches a hand-maintained list of 10 top-level labels (settingsSearchSections.ts). Rows living on the Calendar-look sub-page are invisible, and there are no synonyms — typing "dark" returns nothing even though Theme is one tap away.

2  ·  Locked features visible in place (the revenue one)

Free users currently learn what Premium unlocks from one banner near the footer. The settings they can't use are simply absent, so the value is abstract. Showing them locked-but-visible turns settings into a conversion surface with intent already in the room.

3  ·  Week start day + inline value previews

There is no week-start setting anywhere in the app. For anyone outside the US-Sunday convention, the calendar reads wrong every single day — the highest-frequency papercut a habit calendar can have. Same pass: rows show their current value so you can read state without opening each one.
