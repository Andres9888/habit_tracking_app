Ring Progress
SVG ring around each day cell shows partial completion (e.g. 4/6 habits). Green when complete, amber for today's in-progress.
Chain Connectors
Gradient bars (4px, rounded) connect consecutive complete days. Creates a visual "chain" that you don't want to break.
Celebration State
Perfect day triggers shimmer text, sparkle particles, gradient progress bar animation. Visceral reward for completing all habits.
Thicker Progress Bar
6px bar (was 3px) with percentage label. Gradient fill with highlight edge. Actually visible at a glance.
Future Day Treatment
Dashed border instead of opacity:0.5. Feels inviting and "ready to be filled" rather than dead/disabled.
Today Emphasis
Amber pulsing shadow, ring showing today's progress percentage. The day that matters most gets the strongest visual weight.
