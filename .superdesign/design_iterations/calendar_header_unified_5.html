→ What changed
Removed: ProgressDots. "3 of 6" text already communicates the same data. Six 6px dots add visual weight for zero new information.
Removed: "🔥 Keep going!" badge. The greeting "5-day streak" already carries the emotional signal + loss aversion. The badge was coach-y tone the app doesn't use elsewhere.
Kept: Greeting + fraction. Two distinct data points: emotional context (left) and precise count (right). Scanned in <200ms.

~20px saved vertically — that's half a habit card more visible above the fold.
