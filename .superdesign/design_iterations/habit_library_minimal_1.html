Why minimal wins for users The current screen stacks hero + goal grid + chips + featured + popular before any habit is reachable. This layout shows real habits above the fold with one compact header, one chip row, and one featured pick — fewer decisions, less scrolling, faster comprehension.
Why it wins for monetization Single-tier model → revenue = activation × retention, not upsells. The design optimizes the one metric that matters: time from opening the Library to the first habit added. One-tap Add on every row, an opinionated “Start here” default for paralyzed users, and a quiet inline added-state (green tint, no modal) that keeps people adding instead of dismissing dialogs.
What was cut Tall hero copy, session-progress pill, popularity badges, YouTube icons, and double CTAs (Import + Preview) per card. Science credibility stays as a one-glance “🔬 research-backed” microtext; tapping a row would still open the full preview with citations and tips.
