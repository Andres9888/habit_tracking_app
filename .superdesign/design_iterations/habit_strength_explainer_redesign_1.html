How Strength Works

Habit Strength shows how ingrained your habit has become. It's based on the proven exponential smoothing algorithm.

When You Complete

Each completion increases strength by 5% of the remaining gap to 100%.

When You Miss

Missing a day reduces strength by 5%. Consistent misses weaken the habit.

Building to 100%

With perfect consistency, it takes 60–90 days to reach near 100% strength.

💡 Tips for Building Strength
• Consistency beats intensity — daily small wins add up
• Don't break the chain — each day matters
• If you miss, get back immediately — decay is gradual
• Watch your 30-day comparison to track progress

Tips for building strength

Why these changes
1Mode-aware numbers. The hard-coded 5% claim was wrong after we made the algorithm mode-aware. Now we surface the active mode's actual growth/decay (from ALGORITHM_MODE_CONFIGS) so the modal can't lie. The mode banner up top tells you which set of numbers you're reading.
2Theme tokens for tier colors. Backgrounds and text now come from colors.status.{success,error,warning}Light + STRENGTH_LEVELS, so they flip correctly in dark mode and match the ring/chart tier colors. No more hex.
3Tips card is a real card. Background switched from colors.background (which equals the modal's container) to colors.card with a 1px border. It now has visual weight against the page.
4Updated tips. Tip 3 was misleading after the decay-between-toggles fix — replaced with copy that explains decay runs even when the app is closed. Tip 4 referenced a removed "30-day comparison" UI; updated to point at the 7d/30d deltas the hero now shows.
5"Three intensities" card. The Strength section has an AlgorithmModeChip but the explainer never explained it. Closes the loop: shows all three modes side-by-side with their actual rates, highlights the active one, and invites the user to switch.
6Header echoes section typography. Title uses typography.heading3 for type-ramp consistency with "Habit Strength" in the section. Close button keeps the same shape — minor.
7Removed emoji from headings. Lucide icons throughout matches the section. 💡 lived in tips title only — now a plain heading.
·Out of scope for this pass: bottom safe-area inset (low-risk fix in index.tsx:34), drag handle on Android.
