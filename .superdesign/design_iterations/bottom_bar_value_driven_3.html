Design Decisions — What Changed from V2
Fix Pill ↔ FAB Vertical Alignment
Pills now vertically center with the FAB button using marginTop: -9px on both pill zones. Math: FAB center = -20 + 30 = y10, pill center = -9 + 19 = y10.
container: alignItems: 'flex-start' · leftZone/rightZone: marginTop: -9
Fix Explore Pill: Tinted Glass, Not Solid Gold
Changed from solid streak.600 background with white text to tinted frosted glass. Maintains the bar's frosted glass language while still providing gold warmth.
light: rgba(254,243,205,0.45) + streak.700 text · dark: rgba(139,98,8,0.1) + streak.300 text
Fix Ghost Pill: Faint Glass, Not Invisible
Changed from fully transparent to faint frosted glass. Discoverable but clearly lower in visual hierarchy than the gold Explore pill.
light: rgba(255,255,255,0.35) + stone-400 text · dark: rgba(31,41,55,0.25) + dark-500 text
New Two-Tone Progress Label
The completed count ("2") renders in bold primary[700] green while "of 3" stays in text.tertiary. On all-done, entire label shows "All done!" in completeText.
count: primary[700] (#047857) bold · rest: text.tertiary (#6E6660) · done: primary[600]
Polish Celebration: Glow + Icon Crossfade + Haptic
On all-done transition: ring gets GLOW_SHADOW, + fades out (150ms) → ✓ fades in with BOUNCE_SPRING (d:12, s:200) overshoot, HapticPatterns.celebration() fires, gold pill intensifies with subtle pulse glow.
BOUNCE_SPRING: {d:12, s:200} · crossfade: 150ms · haptic: Light→Medium→Heavy→Success
