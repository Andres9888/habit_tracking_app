Why this is the highest-impact change

Live preview strip (reuses the real ChainDayItem — pattern already proven in the medan worktree). Every toggle below it gives instant feedback. Settings become a play surface instead of a form.

Theme row + High contrast are pure wiring — state and persistence already exist in SettingsModal.hooks.ts. Dark mode is the most-requested habit-app setting; shipping it costs one row.

Rows reordered: visual identity choices (shape, fill, chain) sit directly under the preview they affect.

Preview adds ~90px height to the expanded section — acceptable since sections are collapsible.

Monetization: perceived depth + ease ARE the lever in a single-tier model. A settings page that feels alive sells the product's craftsmanship → conversion & retention.
