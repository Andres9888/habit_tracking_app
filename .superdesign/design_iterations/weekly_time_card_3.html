Tap any day to log time

Tapping Friday: cell scales 0.96, fills with darker tint, ＋ goes accent. Sheet opens for that date.

Behavior summary

  • Empty days — dashed border, tiny ＋ underneath the day letter. Communicates "you can fill me in."
  • Filled days — solid soft tint of the habit color, no ＋. Just shows the value.
  • Today — solid border in the habit's accent color. Filled or empty, today is always anchored.
  • Pressed — cell scales to 0.96 and tint deepens for the duration of the touch (120ms ease).
  • Hint — a single muted line below the row: Tap any day to log time. Removable once we see analytics show people use it.

Code change: ~15 lines in WeeklyTimeBreakdown.tsx + 1 hint line in WeeklyTimeCard.tsx. No behavior change — same onDayPress(date), same sheet.

Cells render at the same height as today's version. Dashed border is 1px rgba(45,42,38,0.14). ＋ is text — no icon swap, no extra dependency.
