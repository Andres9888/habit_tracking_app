How Pattern B compares to Pattern A (skill default)

  • Pattern A (skill default): Welcome → Goal → Pain → Solution → Demo → Paywall (10 screens, goal at #2)
  • Pattern B (this flow): Welcome → Problem → Solution → Goal → Pain → Strength → Demo → Process → Reveal → Paywall (goal at #4)

Why Pattern B fits ChainDay specifically

  • Preserves the narrative moat. "Blind → map" is unique. Most habit apps look like questionnaire clones — Pattern B keeps ChainDay distinctive.
  • Earns the right to ask. "Now you know what we do — what do you want to build?" lands better than "Hi, what's your goal?" cold at screen 2.
  • Sunk cost still happens early enough. Goal at #4 is 6 screens before paywall — plenty of room for commitment to compound.
  • Lower bounce risk. Some users hate being asked things before they understand the app. Story-first reduces that.

The 4 acts mapped to skill archetypes

  • Act 1 Story (1–3) — Welcome (#1) + Problem framing + Personalised Solution preview (#6, universal version)
  • Act 2 Personalize (4–5) — Goal Question (#2) + Pain Points (#3)
  • Act 3 Activate (6–9) — Comparison (#7, strength vs streaks) + App Demo (#11) + Processing (#10) + Value Delivery (#12)
  • Act 4 Commit (10) — Paywall (#14) at emotional peak

Conversion expectations vs current 7-screen flow

  • Onboarding completion: ~3–5% lower (more screens). Industry tradeoff.
  • Paywall conversion: ~50–80% higher (post-value vs pre-value placement).
  • Net trial sign-ups: ~25–40% lift expected.
  • Day-30 retention: ~10–15% higher (sunk cost + first chain link compound retention).
