What's new vs. shipped

1 "From Habit Library" pill — quiet provenance signal under the hero. Only renders if habit.sourceTemplateId is set. Tells the user "the science here came from the template you picked."
2 Section relabel: "Scientific Benefits" — when benefits originate from a template, the section header is "Scientific Benefits" with a leaf glyph. Manually-added benefits keep the plain "Benefits" label.
3 Bullet treatment — circular check chips on white instead of plain dots. Visually elevates the items so they read as evidence rather than a casual list.
4 "Backed by research" attribution panel — replaces the old "Science" prose block. Microscope seal + caption + citation + "View study" link to template.scientificLink. Source: habit.scienceNote (which the importer fills from template.scientificReference).
5 Data work needed — templates currently ship scientificReference + scientificLink but no benefits[] array in the seed data. Implementing this means populating benefits for each template (4 short bullets per habit, derived from the cited study).
