📊 My Recommendation

Option A (1M / 3M / 1Y) provides the best balance:

  • • 1M - Recent progress, immediate feedback
  • • 3M - Habit formation window, quarterly review
  • • 1Y - Long-term trends, sufficient for most users

"All Time" can be accessed via a long-press or settings if needed later.
