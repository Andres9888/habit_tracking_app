⚠ Divergence from mock_4 — the "From Habit Library" pill lives inside the card body (above the personal blocks) instead of floating between the hero and the tabs. This kept the change inside HabitWhyBenefitsCard/ and avoided touching HabitDetailContent.tsx.

2 Personal block — unchanged. Renders any non-empty habit.why, habit.identity, habit.woopWish.
3 "Scientific Benefits" header + leaf glyph — BenefitsList.tsx now takes an isFromTemplate prop. When true: header relabels to "Scientific Benefits" with a Lucide Leaf icon. When false: stays "Benefits" with the standard line+label divider.
4 Circle-check chip bullets — replaces plain dot bullets. White fill, amber border (#FCD34D), Lucide Check icon inside in deep amber (#B45309). Applied for both template and manual benefits — small visual elevation everywhere.
5 "Backed by research" panel — ScienceNoteBlock.tsx swaps the divider label from "Science" to "Backed by research" when a scientificLink is present. Adds a microscope seal (Lucide Microscope on cream-yellow circle) and a "View study" link that opens scientificLink via Linking.openURL.
