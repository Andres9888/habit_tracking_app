Spacing & type Unchanged — padding 20, radius 26, icon 52, title 21/26, desc 3 lines, footer margin 24 I tightened these first; you said you preferred the old spacing, so all metrics were reverted. The density win comes from removing blocks, not from squeezing.

On the card colour: the surface token never changed — still cardPaper #F8F5F1. If it still reads too flat, add depth back deliberately rather than restoring the boxes.
