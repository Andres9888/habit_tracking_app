Drink Water

Promise

Stay hydrated so your energy is steadier through the afternoon.

⏱ 2 min/day Style: Gentle start Difficulty: Easy

Why this works

  • Supports stable concentration during late-morning work.
  • Reduces afternoon fatigue and mindless snacking.
  • Improves workout recovery and perceived effort.

Science: Journal of Nutrition (2019), hydration and cognitive performance. Read reference

How you'll do it

Cue: After I start my coffee, I take 4 sips of water.

Start small: Minimum version is just 4 sips.

Tip: Keep bottle visible on your desk or kitchen counter.

Who you'll become

"I'm someone who takes care of my body before I feel depleted."
