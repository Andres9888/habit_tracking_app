4. Recommendations to lock in max conversion + retention

Critical fixes (highest impact)

  • Wire pickFirstRoute (#7) to actually create the habit. Currently the user emotionally commits but no Convex write happens — they land in empty state and have to repeat the action. This single fix is probably the highest-ROI item in the whole flow.
  • Re-add notificationPriming (#9). Push notifications enabled vs disabled is the single largest retention gap in habit apps. Day-30 retention typically swings 40–60% on this alone.
  • Re-add firstCheckIn (#8) + celebration (#10). Activation moments. The user does the action once before paywall hits — sunk cost + dopamine create the conditions for higher conversion.

Structural moves (high impact)

  • Move auth (#11) from BEFORE onboarding to AFTER activation. Auth-first walls drop 50–80% of organic visitors. Let users see value, do the action, earn link 1 — then ask for sign-in to "save your progress." Conversion rate on auth typically rises 2–3x in this position.
  • Move paywall (#12) from immediate-post-onboarding to post-celebration. Best moment for a paywall is at the emotional peak after a win, not before any commitment.

Optional but worth considering

  • Add social proof somewhere in the flow. "X habits forged today" or a single testimonial. Best placements: between solutionIntro (#3) and strengthHolds (#4), or on the celebration screen (#10).
  • Capture name + goal somewhere. Currently OnboardingAnswers.name and goal are dead fields. Could thread name into screen 4 ("Andres, you won't end up back at zero") for personalization.
  • Adopt copper accent on Hook act. See copper_accent_pattern.html mock — minor but real visual cohesion improvement.

Honest tradeoff to discuss

  • Going from 7 → 13 screens is a +85% length increase. That's the right move ONLY if (a) the new screens are conversion- + retention-positive (they are, based on the impact ratings), and (b) abandonment doesn't spike in the new screens. Realistic data: in well-designed activation flows, longer onboarding with action moments OUTPERFORMS shorter onboarding without them. Headspace, Calm, Duolingo all use this pattern.
