Tradeoffs of the benefit-driven pivot:
  • Wins: Aligned with the video's habit-tracker prescription. Less Q&A friction (5 fewer steps). Lower Apple App Review risk on the paywall. User experiences the product before paying. Each benefit screen is a standalone hook that could also work as a paid-ad creative.
  • Losses: No personalized "Here's what you're getting" beat (we lose the per-pain mapping that solutions.ts provides). No commitment-by-investment effect (questionnaire's classic conversion lever). Soft paywall converts at lower rates initially but retains better.
  • What stays the same: Welcome (1), Problem (2), Solution Intro (3), Social Proof (8), Name (9), Pick Habits ≈ App Demo (10), Plan Preview (11), First Check-In (12), Celebration (13), Notifications (14), Account (15).

The honest call: the video's prescription is benefit-driven for habit trackers. But the questionnaire pattern works too — it's been proven by Prayer Lock, Fastic, Finch, etc. What we have now is workable. The question is whether you want the speed/honesty of benefit-driven or the conversion-by-investment of questionnaire. Both are valid; only one matches the specific advice in the video you shared.
