What Changed & Why

→
Templates color
#7c3aed purple (off-palette) → Warm stone frosted glass — feels like a free helper, matches Settings symmetry Delight
→
Progress counter
"1 done" below FAB → No count text in bar — removes any habit-limit anxiety, keeps focus on action Simplicity
→
Badge dot
Animated green badge → No badge on Templates — no extra notification noise, simpler cognitive load Delight
→
FAB shape
Circle (r=24, 48px) → Squircle (r=16, 52px) — matches card system border radius, larger tap target Engagement
→
Visual symmetry
Mismatched styles (ghost vs solid) → Both pills use frosted glass — balanced, calm navigation Delight
→
Bar surface
Flat rgba overlay → Parchment-tinted blur(20px) — content shows through naturally Delight
→
Typography
System font 12px → DM Sans 13px — matches app caption scale, consistent branding Delight
→
Pill radius
10px (arbitrary) → 12px (design system) — aligns with app's 12px button radius token Delight
Natural Revenue Path (Free-to-3)
Create 3 Free Habits → Build Streaks & Love It → Browse Templates for Ideas → Want a 4th Habit → Tap Add → Upgrade Prompt → RevenueCat Purchase
Design Principle
In free-to-3, the bar should make everything feel free and inviting. The gold/amber "premium discovery" approach from v9 was wrong because it signals friction on a free feature. Instead, both pills use identical warm stone treatment — Templates is a helpful exploration tool, not a paywall surface. Revenue comes from users naturally wanting expansion after falling in love with their free habits.
