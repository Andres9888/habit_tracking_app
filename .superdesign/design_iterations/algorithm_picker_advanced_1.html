Rationale Why this works
Hide by default
Advanced section keeps the create/edit forms calm. Users who don't care about algorithm never see it. Matches the existing settings-accordion pattern.
Labels over icons
Heart/Activity/Zap mean nothing without context. Plain names + outcome sentences remove ambiguity in under a second.
Science-backed framing
UCL research: habits form in 18–254 days (66-day average). Reframing from "penalty strength" to "habit complexity" grounds the choice in evidence, not self-discipline theater.
Smart recommendation
Preview strip detects complexity from the habit name and nudges the right algorithm — "'Meditation' looks Complex. Try Forgiving." Turns an abstract setting into a one-tap decision.
Two-surface strategy
Create/Edit: hide behind Advanced. Settings: compact picker + inline legend (user navigated there deliberately).
