The honest delta: full airy shows ~1 fewer habit per screen and rounds harder. If your users keep 5–8 habits, that's basically free and the calmer look is a net win — go full airy. If you expect power users with long lists, the dense list earns its keep. Partial (shipped) is the hedge: softer + roomier Settings, but the list stays tight.
