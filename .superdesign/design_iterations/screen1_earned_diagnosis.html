How to read this and what to do next

  1. Read each pair like a stranger seeing the app for the first time. Which screen-1 opener makes screen-2's "blind" punch feel earned?
  2. My ranked picks for conversion: A (category truth) > B (behavior mirror) > D (counter-narrative) > C (universal) > E (stat). A is the safest big win. B is the highest ceiling but riskiest. D is the most defensible against streak-app competitors.
  3. For an A/B test: ship A vs current first — it's the lowest-effort change with the cleanest measurable impact (drop-off between screen 1 → 2).
  4. Once a winner is picked I'll wire it into WelcomeStep.tsx with the right type ramp (current 72pt is too big for these copy lengths — needs ~38–44pt for the 2-line variants).
