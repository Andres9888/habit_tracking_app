Variation 1: Current (Fade Up)
Morning Meditation
Daily at 7:00 AM

Current behavior: Entire card fades up together. The accent bar appears instantly with the card, which can feel abrupt when transitioning from the empty state.

Variation 2: Accent Slides Down First Recommended
Morning Meditation
Daily at 7:00 AM

Accent bar leads: The green accent slides down first (150ms), then content fades in from left (300ms). Creates visual hierarchy and draws attention to the color coding.

Variation 3: Accent Grows from Center
Morning Meditation
Daily at 7:00 AM

Center expansion: Accent bar grows outward from center, then content reveals. More playful feel, good for celebratory moments.

Variation 4: Slide from Left
Morning Meditation
Daily at 7:00 AM

Directional entrance: Card slides in from left, accent bar acts as the "leading edge". Creates strong directional flow but may feel too aggressive.

Variation 5: Ghost Card → Accent → Content
Morning Meditation
Daily at 7:00 AM

Three-stage reveal: Ghost card outline appears first, accent bar fills in, then content fades. Sophisticated but slower (500ms total).

Variation 6: Accent Bar Expands Width
Morning Meditation
Daily at 7:00 AM

Width growth: Card fades in, then accent bar grows from 0 to 6px width. Subtle but adds polish without being distracting.

Recommendations

1. Variation 2 (Accent Slides Down First) - Best balance of visual interest and performance. The accent bar leading creates a natural visual anchor and emphasizes the color-coding feature. Duration: ~400ms total.

2. Variation 6 (Width Expansion) - More subtle alternative. Good if you want minimal disruption to existing animation flow. The width animation is quick and adds polish without being distracting.

Avoid: Variation 4 (Slide from Left) can feel too aggressive for a habit list, and Variation 5 adds too much latency for a common interaction.
