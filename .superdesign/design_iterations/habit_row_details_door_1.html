Where this applies

Every habit surface gets the same anatomy: popular rows on the main page, prescription step rows ("Why it works ›" replaces the bare reason text's citation), goal-page rows, and catalog cards (the pill sits under the name, replacing one meta pill). When a template has a real citation we show "🔬 Basso et al. (2019) ›" — author names build more trust than a generic label; when it doesn't, the pill reads "🔬 Why it works ›". Either way it's visibly a button and lands on the details page with the science card at top.
