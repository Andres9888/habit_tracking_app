ChainDay — max conversion flow (10 screens, skill-driven)

Built using the app-onboarding-questionnaire skill's 14-archetype framework, optimized for max conversion. Goal: hit every required archetype + the highest-impact recommended ones, in the leanest sequence. Each screen earns its conversion weight. Goes from 7 narrative screens → 10 questionnaire screens. Industry data: 8–11 onboarding screens is the sweet spot for top subscription apps (Cal AI, Noom, Headspace) — fewer kills sunk cost, more kills throughput.

🎯 Why this 10-screen flow maximizes conversion

  • 3 sunk-cost levers: goal pick (#2), habit creation (#7), chain reveal (#9). Current 7-screen has 0 of these — user reaches paywall with no possessions.
  • Personalization throughout — screens 5–7 mirror back what user said in 2 + 3. Current flow is one-size-fits-all.
  • Paywall at emotional peak — after the chain reveal. Industry: ~50–80% higher conversion vs current position (post-onboarding, pre-value).
  • Social proof — current flow has zero. Adding it reduces "is this for real?" risk perception.
  • App demo is functional — current pickFirstRoute doesn't actually create the habit. This fixes the highest-leverage gap in the entire flow.

  • − Loses "narrative purity" of current flow. Becomes a questionnaire-style flow (proven pattern, less distinctive).
