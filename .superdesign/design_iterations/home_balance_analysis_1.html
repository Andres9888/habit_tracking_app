Balance Issues + Recommendations

1. Padding Misalignment (20px vs 16px)
Timeline uses px-5 (20px), cards use 16px margin. The left column shows the 4px offset — day strip edges don't align with card edges. Fix: change CalendarTimeline from px-5 to px-4 (16px).
2. No Visual Transition
Without the card wrapper, the timeline and habit cards blur together. A subtle gradient divider (middle/right columns) provides a clean rhythm break between "context" (timeline) and "action" (habits).
3. Ring Size Trade-off
64px ring (middle) is prominent and motivating but pushes the first card ~20px lower. 52px ring (right) saves ~16px vertical space while remaining clearly readable. Recommended: 64px for max engagement.
4. Card Surface Contrast
White cards (#FFFFFF) on warm canvas (#F5F1ED) create proper depth hierarchy. The borderless timeline correctly defers to cards as the primary interactive surface. No change needed.
