Chain Day · Settings IA Rethink

Before & After

Today's Settings is a flat 6-section modal. It reads cleanly but has real information-architecture friction: account actions are buried two taps deep, sort is the lone navigate-away picker, the list is a long scroll with no fast path, and Archived / Export hide inside "Behavior." The three redesigns each fix all four — the difference is how. Scroll right to compare.
