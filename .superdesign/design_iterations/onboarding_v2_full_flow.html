The "why" pattern across the flow

Screen 2 plants a metaphor (blind path). Screen 3 promises a fix (map) + seeds the 3 ideas. Screens 4–6 each unpack one of those ideas. Screen 7 collects the commit. Every Act 2 screen pays off one promise from screen 3, so the flow feels coherent rather than like a list of features.
