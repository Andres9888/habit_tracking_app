Privacy Policy for Chain Day

Last Updated: February 3, 2026

Welcome to Chain Day ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in an open and transparent manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our habit tracking mobile application ("Chain Day" or the "App").

By using Chain Day, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

Email Address: When you create an account through Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In via Clerk authentication, we collect your email address for account creation and authentication purposes.

Usage Data

Habit Data: We collect and store the habits you create, including:

  • Habit names and descriptions
  • Habit categories and settings
  • Completion tracking data (dates and times you mark habits as complete)
  • Habit streaks and statistics
  • Custom reminders and notifications settings

Automatically Collected Information

Device Information: We may collect information about your device, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • App version

Error and Performance Data: Through Sentry, we collect crash reports and performance metrics to improve app stability and user experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To create and manage your account, authenticate you, and provide account recovery options
  • Core Functionality: To sync your habit data across devices and provide the habit tracking features
  • Service Improvement: To analyze app usage, fix bugs, and improve performance
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide technical support
  • Subscription Management: To process and manage your subscription payments through RevenueCat
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party services to provide and improve Chain Day:

Clerk (Authentication)

  • Purpose: User authentication via Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In
  • Data Shared: Email address, authentication tokens
  • Privacy Policy: https://clerk.com/legal/privacy

Convex (Backend & Cloud Sync)

  • Purpose: Backend infrastructure and real-time data synchronization
  • Data Shared: Email address, habit data, completion tracking data
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.convex.dev/privacy

Sentry (Error Tracking)

  • Purpose: Error monitoring and performance tracking
  • Data Shared: Device information, error logs, performance metrics (no personal habit data)
  • Privacy Policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

  • Purpose: Managing in-app subscriptions and payments
  • Data Shared: User identifier, subscription status, purchase information
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Data Storage and Security

Cloud Storage

Your data is stored securely on Convex's cloud infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Encryption at rest for stored data
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication requirements

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. Specifically:

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Habit Data: Retained while your account is active and for 30 days after account deletion (to allow for account recovery)
  • Error Logs: Retained for up to 90 days
  • Subscription Data: Retained as required by law for tax and accounting purposes (typically 7 years)
