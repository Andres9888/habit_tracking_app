Where streaks live

A streak is a run of days in the log, not a grade. Habit Detail is a recommitment surface — it must not show a streak, and recovery must not say “you broke N.” Home keeps a quiet current number after a tick. Analytics holds current + longest, as facts you can audit on History.

Why this change

Pain
🔥 + “12 Days Streak” + a medal turns the check-off row into a scoreboard. Best-streak chrome belongs in a look session, not next to the tick.
Outcome
After you log, a small current number is enough pride. Zero run shows nothing — no shame badge.
Not this
No fire emoji, no “New Record!”, no best on the card. Longest lives on Analytics.

04 · Do not put streaks here

04

Wrong rooms

Detail recovery

“Yesterday broke a 12-day streak.” Amber already means start again. Accusing with N makes the at-risk person leave.

History header

A giant “12-day streak” on the calendar. History owns dates. The run is visible as consecutive filled days — don’t title the month with a score.

Settings

Streak reminders can stay as a nudge toggle. Settings is not a scoreboard.
