Who owns what

Home / Today

Owns fast check-off. A person who just wants to tick the box never has to open Detail.

Habit Detail

Owns the current action, one small trustworthy snapshot, and calm recovery. It is a recommitment surface: name, why, strength dial, one primary action, this week, and two doors out. Past-date editing deliberately does not live here.

History

Owns the record — every date, note, and correction. This is the only place a past day can be edited, which keeps Detail focused on today.

Analytics

Owns patterns and their evidence. Each insight is tappable so the claim can be audited against the logged dates behind it.

Design notes

  • Amber is reserved. The warm amber panel appears only in the recovery state, so its presence itself carries meaning.
  • The rows never move. The action area is a fixed-height slot in all three Detail states, so History and Analytics stay in exactly the same place whether or not today is complete.
  • The dial is a snapshot, not a rank. It reads 42 · Building with no percentage sign, no leaderboard, and no comparison to other people.
  • Calendar states are legible without colour. Filled disc, dashed ring, solid ring, and flat fill distinguish completed, no entry, today, and upcoming — with a legend below.
  • No rest days, no streak shaming. A miss is described factually and answered with a smaller version of the habit.
  • Insights are auditable. Every insight row opens a screen showing the counts and dates behind it, plus an explicit note that a pattern is not a cause.
