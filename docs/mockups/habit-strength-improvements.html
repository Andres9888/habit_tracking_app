Habit Strength section — how to make it more motivating

The current Strength card answers "what's my number?" well, but doesn't tell the user where they're going, how close they are, or what's protecting their progress. The proposal below adds a tiered milestone tracker, a forecast curve, an insight chip, and surfaces the streak shield — all without making the section taller on mobile.

Variant explorations

Pick / mix the pieces that fit best
VARIANT A

Growth ladder

Replace the ring with a 5-step growth glyph. Each step is a "tier" (Seed → Sprout → Sapling → Strong → Unstoppable). Communicates direction of travel at a glance — no math needed.
