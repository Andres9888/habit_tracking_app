✅ Borrow from Duolingo

  • Chain continuity you can read at a glance (circles + joins)
  • One primary complete control that feels “settled” when done
  • Streak as the hero story; prev best as a finish line
  • Milestone only on rare crossings — calm line, not confetti storm
  • De-dupe numbers (no twin “12 / 12”, no triple strength UI)

🚫 Still not Duolingo

  • No mascot, XP bar, hearts, or league chrome
  • No rainbow saturation or cartoon type
  • No party on every tap — everyday = settle, rare = one beat
  • No new colors outside forest / streak gold / parchment
  • No decorative idle loops or constant bounce
Duolingo mechanics · Chain Day craft Design system owns tokens, motion budget, and components. Duolingo only influences what progress should feel like on the daily path.
