Design System Specifications

  • Typography: 34pt (counts), 22pt (titles), 17pt (body), 13pt (captions)
  • Font Family: SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text (system default)
  • Primary Green: #047857 (light mode) / #10b981 (dark mode)
  • Border Radius: 16px (widgets), 12px (buttons), 6px (checkboxes)
  • Shadows: 4px offset, 16px blur, 0.08 opacity
  • Widget Sizes: Small 169×169px, Medium 360×169px, Lock Screen 160×160px (circular)
  • Update Frequency: Every 15 minutes or on app background
  • Data Source: Shared App Group (UserDefaults)
