## User Story

**As a** user,
**I want** an updated home page with a modern design featuring a date selector, visual habit chains, emoji support for habit icons, and streak information,
**so that** I can easily track my habits across multiple days, personalize my habit experience with custom emojis, and stay motivated by seeing my progress visually.

**As a** developer,
**I want** comprehensive test coverage, clean code, and accessibility features,
**so that** the implementation is maintainable, reliable, and usable by all people including those with disabilities.
