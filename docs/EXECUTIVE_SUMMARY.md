## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Challenges

**Challenge 1: Lower than expected conversion**
- **Risk Level**: Medium
- **Mitigation**: A/B test pricing ($3.99 vs $4.99 vs $5.99)
- **Fallback**: Extend trial to 14 days, add more premium features

**Challenge 2: Negative user feedback on paywall**
- **Risk Level**: Low
- **Mitigation**: Keep free tier generous, never paywall core features
- **Fallback**: Adjust messaging, emphasize value over price

**Challenge 3: Technical complexity delays launch**
- **Risk Level**: Low
- **Mitigation**: Use proven solutions (RevenueCat), feature flags for gradual rollout
- **Fallback**: Ship features incrementally

**Challenge 4: Gamification doesn't engage users**
- **Risk Level**: Medium
- **Mitigation**: Make it optional, test with beta group first
- **Fallback**: Focus on other retention tactics (social, notifications)

## FAQ

**Q: Why not do everything at once?**  
A: Focus beats scope. Ship fast, learn fast, iterate. The 4 quick wins (12 hours) will have more impact than 100 hours of "nice to have" features.

**Q: What if users don't want to pay?**  
A: Our free tier is generous. Premium is for power users who get massive value. 5-7% conversion is industry standard, and we're offering genuine value (analytics, predictions).

**Q: Should we add ads to free tier?**  
A: Not yet. Build a loved product first. Ads hurt retention and ratings. Focus on premium conversion. Consider ads only if conversion fails.

**Q: What about international markets?**  
A: Start with English. Once you hit $50K MRR, localize to Spanish, Portuguese, French. Language is a multiplier, not a starting point.

**Q: How do we compete with free apps?**  
A: Quality over quantity. Your predictions and analytics are unique. Most habit apps are simple checklists. You're building behavior science into software.

**Q: Should we pivot to B2B?**  
A: B2C first. Build a product individuals love, then package for teams. Enterprise sales are long and complex. Bootstrap with consumer revenue.
