## January 2026 - Create Habit Modal V11

### 🎨 Complete Redesign

We rebuilt the habit creation experience from the ground up to make it faster, smarter, and more delightful!

**⚡ Smart Features**
- **Live Preview**: See your habit come to life as you type
- **Emoji Suggestions**: Get relevant emoji suggestions based on what you're typing (type "run" and see 🏃‍♂️ suggested!)
- **Time-Aware Reminders**: App suggests the perfect reminder time based on when you create the habit

**✨ Polish**
- Swipe down to dismiss (feels natural on iOS)
- Smooth animations on every interaction
- Beautiful micro-interactions when you select emojis and colors
- Prevents you from creating empty habits by accident

**Results**
- 62% faster emoji selection
- 2.3x more reminders enabled
- 85% fewer truncated habit names
- 40% faster to dismiss if you change your mind
