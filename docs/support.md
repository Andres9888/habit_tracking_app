## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

#### How do I create a new habit?

1. Tap the **"+" button** on the home screen or in the habits list
2. Enter your habit name (e.g., "Morning workout")
3. Choose your frequency:
   - **Daily**: Track every day
   - **Weekly**: Set specific days (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri)
   - **Custom**: Set your own schedule
4. (Optional) Add a description, set reminders, or customize the icon
5. Tap **"Create"** to save

Your new habit will appear on your home screen, ready to track!

#### How do I mark a habit as complete?

- **Tap the habit card** on your home screen
- Or **swipe right** on the habit for quick check-in
- The checkmark will appear, and your streak will update

**Note**: You can only check in once per day for daily habits.

#### Can I edit a habit after creating it?

Yes!

1. Tap on the habit card to open details
2. Tap the **edit icon** (pencil) in the top-right
3. Modify any settings (name, frequency, icon, etc.)
4. Tap **"Save"** to confirm

### Streaks

#### How do streaks work?

A **streak** is the number of consecutive times you've completed a habit according to its schedule.

- **Daily habits**: Complete the habit every day to maintain your streak
- **Weekly habits**: Complete on all scheduled days each week
- **Custom schedules**: Follow your custom frequency

**Example**: If you have a daily habit and complete it today and tomorrow, you'll have a 2-day streak. Miss a day, and your streak resets to 0.

#### Why did my streak reset?

Your streak resets when you miss a scheduled completion:

- **Daily habits**: Missing any day resets the streak
- **Weekly habits**: Missing a scheduled day resets the streak
- **Grace period**: We don't provide grace periods to maintain accountability

**Tip**: Set notifications to remind yourself before the day ends!

#### Can I recover a lost streak?

Currently, streaks cannot be manually recovered. However:

- Your **longest streak** is always saved and displayed
- Your **total completions** are preserved
- You can start a new streak immediately

We're considering a "freeze" feature for premium users—stay tuned!

#### What's the difference between current streak and longest streak?

- **Current Streak**: How many times in a row you've completed the habit recently
- **Longest Streak**: Your all-time best consecutive streak for this habit

Both are displayed on your habit card to track progress!

### Subscriptions & Payments

#### What's included in the free version?

The free version includes:

- Up to **3 active habits**
- Basic streak tracking
- Daily check-ins
- Simple notifications

#### What do I get with Premium?

Premium unlocks:

- **Unlimited habits**
- Advanced habit scheduling
- Detailed statistics and insights
- Habit templates
- Custom themes and icons
- Priority support
- Export your data
- Ad-free experience

#### How much does Premium cost?

- **Monthly Plan**: $4.99/month
- **Annual Plan**: $39.99/year (save 33%!)

Prices may vary by region and are displayed in your local currency.

#### How do I subscribe to Premium?

1. Tap the **"Upgrade"** button in the app
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual** plan
3. Confirm through the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android)
4. Premium features activate immediately

#### How do I cancel my subscription?

**iPhone/iPad:**

1. Open **Settings** → Tap your **Apple ID** at the top
2. Tap **Subscriptions**
3. Find **Chain Day**
4. Tap **Cancel Subscription**

**Android:**

1. Open **Google Play Store**
2. Tap **Menu** (☰) → **Subscriptions**
3. Find **Chain Day**
4. Tap **Cancel Subscription**

**Important**: You must cancel at least 24 hours before your next renewal to avoid being charged.

#### Will I get a refund if I cancel?

- Subscriptions are **non-refundable**
- You'll retain Premium access until the end of your current billing period
- No pro-rated refunds for mid-cycle cancellations

Refund requests should be directed to Apple or Google, as they process all payments.

#### How do I restore my Premium purchase on a new device?

1. Open Chain Day on your new device
2. Log in with the **same account** you used to subscribe
3. Go to **Settings** → **Restore Purchases**
4. Your Premium status will sync automatically

**Note**: Make sure you're logged in with the same Apple ID (iOS) or Google account (Android) that you used to purchase Premium.

### Account & Data

#### How do I delete my account?

1. Open **Settings** in the app
2. Scroll to **Account**
3. Tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm deletion

**Warning**: This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your habits, streaks, and data will be deleted within 30 days.

Alternatively, email privacy@chainday.app with "Delete Account" in the subject line.

#### Can I export my data?

Yes! Premium users can export their habit data:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Data & Privacy**
2. Tap **Export My Data**
3. Choose format (CSV or JSON)
4. Download or email the file to yourself

**Free users**: Email support@chainday.app to request a manual data export.

#### How do I sync my habits across devices?

Habits automatically sync when you're logged in:

- Sign in with the **same account** on all devices
- Ensure you have an **internet connection**
- Data syncs in real-time when online
- Offline changes sync when you reconnect

If sync isn't working, try:

1. Pull down to refresh on the home screen
2. Log out and log back in
3. Check your internet connection
4. Contact support if issues persist

#### Is my data private and secure?

Yes! We take your privacy seriously:

- All data is **encrypted in transit and at rest**
- We **never sell your data** to third parties
- Only you can see your habits and streaks
- See our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for full details

## Troubleshooting

### App Issues

#### The app won't open or keeps crashing

1. **Force close** the app completely
2. **Restart** your device
3. **Update** to the latest version from the App Store/Play Store
4. **Reinstall** the app (your data is saved in the cloud)
5. If the issue persists, email bugs@chainday.app with your device model and OS version

#### My habits aren't loading

1. **Check your internet connection** (WiFi or cellular data)
2. **Pull down** on the home screen to manually refresh
3. **Log out and log back in**: Settings → Account → Log Out
4. Ensure you're using the correct account credentials
5. Contact support if habits still don't appear

#### Check-ins not saving

1. Verify you have an **active internet connection**
2. The app saves changes locally first, then syncs when online
3. Try **force-closing** and reopening the app
4. Check if the habit is properly configured (Settings → Edit Habit)
5. Report persistent issues to support@chainday.app

#### Notifications aren't working

**iPhone:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Chain Day** → **Notifications**
2. Enable **Allow Notifications**
3. Choose notification style (banner, alert, etc.)
4. In the Chain Day app, verify notification times are set correctly

**Android:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Apps** → **Chain Day** → **Notifications**
2. Enable notifications and set priority to **High** or **Urgent**
3. Check Battery Optimization settings—Chain Day should be exempt
4. In the Chain Day app, verify notification times are set correctly

#### Premium features not activating after purchase

1. Go to **Settings** → **Restore Purchases**
2. Ensure you're logged in with the **same account** used to purchase
3. Check your subscription status in the App Store/Play Store
4. Wait a few minutes and restart the app
5. Email support@chainday.app with your purchase receipt

### Sync Issues

#### My data isn't syncing between devices

1. Ensure you're **logged into the same account** on all devices
2. Check that all devices have an **active internet connection**
3. **Force a sync**: Pull down on the home screen to refresh
4. Try **logging out and back in** on the problem device
5. Verify you're using the **latest app version** on all devices

#### I see duplicate habits

This can happen if sync conflicts occur:

1. **Don't delete** duplicates immediately
2. Note which habits have the correct data
3. Email support@chainday.app with screenshots
4. We can help resolve the conflict and preserve your data

#### Lost data after reinstalling the app

Your data should be safe in the cloud:

1. **Log in** with the same account you used previously
2. Pull down to **refresh** and sync
3. Wait a few minutes for data to load
4. If data is still missing, contact support@chainday.app immediately

We may be able to recover your data from backups.

### Billing Issues

#### I was charged but don't have Premium access

1. Verify your purchase in the **App Store** (iOS) or **Play Store** (Android)
2. Go to **Settings** → **Restore Purchases** in Chain Day
3. Restart the app and check again
4. If still not working, email support@chainday.app with:
   - Your purchase receipt/order number
   - Device type and OS version
   - When you purchased Premium

#### I was charged twice

Payment processing is handled by Apple/Google:

- Check your **App Store/Play Store purchase history** to confirm duplicate charges
- If you see duplicates, contact **Apple Support** or **Google Play Support** directly
- They can investigate and issue refunds for erroneous charges
- We cannot process refunds directly (payments go through the app stores)

#### How do I change my payment method?

Update your payment method through your device's app store settings:

**iOS**: Settings → Apple ID → Payment & Shipping  
**Android**: Google Play Store → Menu → Payment Methods

Changes apply to all app subscriptions, including Chain Day.
