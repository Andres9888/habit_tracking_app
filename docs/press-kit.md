**Last Updated:** February 2026

## App Description

Chain Day is a habit tracking app that makes consistency visible. Unlike traditional habit trackers that display progress as simple checkmarks or streak numbers, Chain Day visualizes your habits as growing chains — each completed day connects to the last, creating a visual story of your progress. The app also features a unique "Habit Strength" score that measures how established each habit is over time, going beyond simple streak counting to show which behaviors are truly becoming part of your routine. Built with offline support and a minimal, focused design, Chain Day helps users build lasting habits without the gamification tricks or bloat common in the category.

---

## Founder Story

Chain Day was built by Andres, a solo indie developer who spent years struggling with habit consistency. After trying dozens of habit apps that all felt like "glorified spreadsheets," he set out to create something different — a tool that would make progress visible and motivating, not just trackable.

"Every app I tried gave me the same thing: a checkbox and a streak number. But I wanted to _see_ my progress. To feel the momentum of a growing chain. To understand which habits were actually sticking versus which ones I just hadn't broken yet."

The project started as a personal tool and evolved over 8 months of spare-time development into a polished iOS app. The goal was simple: build the habit tracker the founder wished existed — beautiful, focused, and genuinely helpful for personal growth.

Chain Day is self-funded and independently developed, with no venture capital or corporate ownership. It's built by someone who genuinely cares about habit formation, not by a company optimizing for engagement metrics.

## Key Features

### 🔗 Chain Visualization

- Visual representation of streaks as connected chains
- Watch your habits grow link by link
- Leverages the proven "don't break the chain" motivation method
- More motivating than simple streak numbers

### 💪 Habit Strength Score

- Proprietary algorithm that measures habit establishment
- Accounts for consistency over time, not just current streak
- Helps users understand which habits are truly becoming part of their routine
- Differentiates between new streaks and maintained habits

### 📴 Full Offline Support

- Complete habits without internet connection
- Automatic sync when back online
- No lost progress when traveling or disconnected

### 🎨 Intentional Design

- Clean, minimal interface
- No distracting gamification or manipulative mechanics
- Focused on clarity and usability
- Beautiful animations and micro-interactions

### ⏰ Time-Based Suggestions

- Smart habit suggestions based on time of day
- Morning habits shown in the morning, evening habits in the evening
- Reduces friction and decision fatigue

### 📊 Progress Tracking

- Week and calendar views
- Detailed habit history
- Notes for each habit
- Insightful analytics

### 🎮 Optional Gamification

- Character screen with XP and levels
- Achievements and milestone celebrations
- Can be ignored if not desired

### 🔐 Privacy & Security

- Secure authentication via Clerk
- Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In support
- Data encrypted in transit and at rest
