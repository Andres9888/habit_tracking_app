# Chain Day Press Kit

## One-Paragraph App Description
Chain Day is a habit tracking app that helps people build consistency by making progress visible. Instead of treating habits like simple checklists, Chain Day turns streaks into visual chains so users can see momentum build over time. The app also includes a Habit Strength score that reflects long-term consistency, plus weekly/calendar views and offline support. Built with a clean, focused design, Chain Day is made for people who want better habits without clutter, guilt loops, or overcomplicated workflows.

---

## Founder Story
Chain Day was created by a solo indie developer building tools for personal growth. After trying many habit apps and repeatedly falling into all-or-nothing cycles, the founder wanted a tracker that felt motivating and human — not just another list of checkboxes. The project started as a personal experiment: what if consistency could be seen as a growing chain, and progress could be measured as strength over time rather than perfection? That idea became Chain Day, built iteratively through real-world use, feedback, and a focus on thoughtful UX.

---

## Key Features
- **Visual Chain Tracking**: See streaks as connected links to reinforce momentum.
- **Habit Strength Score**: Measure how established a habit is based on consistency over time.
- **Weekly + Calendar Views**: Review patterns and progress at a glance.
- **Offline Support**: Track habits without connection; sync when back online.
- **Time-Based Suggestions**: Contextual suggestions based on time of day.
- **Notes per Habit**: Add context and reflection to each habit.
- **Focused UI**: Clean, minimal design built to reduce friction.
