## 4. Description (4000 characters max)

```
Chain Day makes habit building visual, motivating, and simple.

Most habit trackers give you a checkbox and a number. Chain Day shows you a growing chain — each completed day connects to the last, creating a visual story of your consistency. It's based on the proven "don't break the chain" method, and it works.

WHY CHAIN DAY?

🔗 VISUAL CHAIN TRACKING
Watch your habits grow link by link. The chain visualization turns abstract progress into something you can see and feel. Every day you show up, the chain gets longer. Miss a day? You'll feel it — and that's the point.

💪 HABIT STRENGTH SCORE
Streaks don't tell the whole story. Chain Day's Habit Strength score measures how established each habit truly is over time. A 5-day streak on a brand new habit is different from a 5-day streak on something you've done for months. Now you can see the difference.

📴 WORKS OFFLINE
Life doesn't always come with Wi-Fi. Complete your habits anywhere — on a plane, on a hike, in the subway. Chain Day syncs automatically when you're back online. No lost progress, ever.

⏰ SMART TIME-BASED SUGGESTIONS
Morning habits appear in the morning. Evening habits appear in the evening. Chain Day reduces decision fatigue by surfacing the right habits at the right time.

📊 PROGRESS YOU CAN FEEL
Week view, calendar view, detailed history, and personal notes for each habit. Track not just what you did, but how it felt and why it mattered.

🎮 OPTIONAL GAMIFICATION
Earn XP, level up your character, and unlock achievements — or ignore it entirely. Chain Day adapts to your style. No manipulative dark patterns, no guilt trips.

🔐 PRIVATE & SECURE
Sign in with Apple or Google. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. No ads, no selling your data. Your habits are your business.

BUILT FOR PEOPLE WHO CARE ABOUT CONSISTENCY

Chain Day was built by a solo indie developer who tried every habit app and found them all lacking. No venture capital. No engagement hacks. Just a focused, beautiful tool designed to help you build the life you want, one day at a time.

Whether you're building a meditation practice, hitting the gym, reading daily, drinking more water, or any other habit — Chain Day gives you the visual motivation to keep going.

Start your chain today. Don't break it.

CHAIN DAY PREMIUM:
```
