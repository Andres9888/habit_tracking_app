Chain Day helps you build lasting habits with the power of visual progress tracking. Experience the motivating "don't break the chain" method like never before.

**Why Chain Day Works:**

- 🔗 **Visual Chain Tracking** - Watch your streaks grow day by day. Each completed habit creates a link in your chain, making consistency beautifully visible.

- 📊 **Habit Strength Science** - Our algorithm calculates how established each habit truly is based on frequency, recency, and consistency. See which habits are solid foundations and which need more work.

- ⏰ **Smart Time-Based Suggestions** - Get habit recommendations that match your time of day. Morning routines suggestions appear in the AM, evening habits at night. Contextual tips that actually help.

- 🎮 **Gamified Character Growth** - Earn XP, level up, and unlock achievements as you complete habits. Your character grows alongside your habits, making the journey fun and rewarding.

- 📅 **Flexible Views** - Choose between week view for quick daily planning or calendar view for long-term pattern analysis.

- 📴 **Offline Support** - Complete habits even without internet. Your data syncs automatically when you're back online.

**Perfect for:**
- Fitness habits (exercise, running, yoga)
- Mindfulness (meditation, journaling, gratitude)
- Learning (reading, language practice, skills)
- Productivity (deep work, planning, organization)
- Health (water intake, sleep, nutrition)

Download Chain Day today and start building chains that matter.

# Chain Day Support

Welcome to Chain Day support! We're here to help you build lasting habits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create a habit?
1. Open the app and tap the "+" button
2. Enter your habit name (e.g., "Morning Run")
3. Choose your reminder time (optional)
4. Tap "Create" to start your chain!

### How does the chain work?
Every day you complete a habit, a link is added to your chain. The longer your chain, the more motivated you'll be not to break it!

### What is Habit Strength?
Habit Strength is our scientific way of measuring how established a habit is. It considers:
- How consistently you've completed the habit
- How recently you started
- How often you do it

Stronger habits have higher "strength" and are harder to break!

### How do I earn XP and level up?
You earn XP (experience points) every time you complete a habit. Level up your character by:
- Completing habits daily
- Maintaining long streaks
- Achieving milestones

### Does the app work offline?
Yes! Chain Day works offline. Complete your habits without internet, and your data will sync automatically when you're back online.

### How do I reset a habit?
Go to the habit details screen and tap "Reset Progress." This will clear all completions but keep the habit.

### Can I delete my account?
Yes. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will permanently remove all your data.

We're typically responsive within 24-48 hours.
