## Description (4000 chars max)

Build habits that stick — one chain at a time.

Chain Day uses the proven "Don't Break the Chain" method to help you build lasting habits with visual streak chains, momentum tracking, and smart reminders. Whether you're starting a morning routine, building a fitness habit, or learning a new skill, Chain Day gives you the tools and motivation to stay consistent.

■ THE CHAIN METHOD — PROVEN & VISUAL
See your streaks grow as unbreakable chains. Each day you complete a habit adds a link. The longer the chain, the more motivated you are to keep going. It's the same technique used by Jerry Seinfeld to write every single day — and it works.

■ MOMENTUM STRENGTH SYSTEM
Unlike other trackers that treat Day 1 and Day 100 the same, Chain Day recognizes that habits get stronger over time. Watch your momentum build from fragile to unbreakable as you stay consistent. Miss a day? Your momentum absorbs the impact — because real life isn't perfect, and your tracker shouldn't punish you for it.

■ START IN SECONDS WITH TEMPLATES
Browse our curated library of habit templates — from morning routines and fitness goals to mindfulness practices and study schedules. Tap to add, customize if you want, and start tracking immediately. No setup friction.

■ DAILY PROGRESS RING
See your entire day at a glance. The progress ring fills as you complete habits, giving you instant visual feedback and a satisfying sense of accomplishment. It's the daily dashboard you'll actually want to check.

■ CELEBRATE YOUR WINS
Hit a milestone? Chain Day celebrates with you. Custom animations mark your 7-day, 30-day, 100-day, and yearly streaks. Because building habits is hard — and every milestone deserves recognition.

■ SMART REMINDERS
Set custom notification times for each habit. Chain Day nudges you at the right moment so you never forget. Gentle, helpful, never annoying.

■ ANALYTICS DASHBOARD (PREMIUM)
Dive deep into your habit data with beautiful charts and insights. See completion rates, best days, longest streaks, and trends over time. Understand your patterns and optimize your routines.

■ WHY CHAIN DAY?
• Visual chains make progress tangible and addictive
• Momentum system rewards consistency without punishing imperfection
• Clean, beautiful design you'll enjoy using every day
• Templates get you started in seconds
• No account required — your data stays on your device

■ WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT
Habitica gamifies habits but adds complexity. Streaks tracks but doesn't build momentum. Done counts but doesn't motivate. Chain Day combines the best — visual motivation, momentum science, and beautiful simplicity — into one app that actually helps you change.
