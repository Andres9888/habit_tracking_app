# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 14, 2026**

## Introduction

Welcome to Chain Day ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Chain Day mobile application (the "App").

By using Chain Day, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Data You Provide

When you use Chain Day, we collect and store the following information:

- **Habits and Goals**: The habits you create, including names, descriptions, frequency settings, and custom configurations
- **Streak Data**: Your daily check-ins, streak counts, completion history, and progress metrics
- **App Settings**: Your preferences, notification settings, theme choices, and other customization options
- **Account Information**: Email address and basic profile information (collected through Clerk authentication)

### Automatically Collected Data

- **Usage Analytics**: App interactions, feature usage, and performance metrics to improve the app experience
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information
- **Performance Data**: Crash reports and diagnostic information to identify and fix bugs

## How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

- Provide and maintain the App's core functionality
- Track your habits and calculate streaks
- Sync your data across devices
- Process subscription payments and manage your account
- Send you notifications about your habits and streaks
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Provide customer support
- Comply with legal obligations

### Analytics

We collect anonymous usage data to understand how users interact with the app and identify areas for improvement. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols
- Data at rest is stored securely on Convex's infrastructure with encryption
- We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your data
- Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

- **With your consent**: When you explicitly agree to share data
- **Service providers**: With third-party services listed above, solely to operate the App
- **Legal requirements**: When required by law, court order, or legal process
- **Business transfers**: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (users will be notified)
- **Safety and protection**: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Chain Day, our users, or others

## Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

### Access and Portability

- Request a copy of your personal data
- Export your habit data and history

### Correction and Deletion

- Update or correct your account information within the App
- Request deletion of your account and all associated data

### GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you're in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under GDPR:

- Right to access your personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent

### CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you're a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
- Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
- Right to deletion
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

## Data Retention

- **Active accounts**: We retain your data as long as your account is active
- **Deleted accounts**: When you delete your account, we permanently remove all personal data within 30 days
- **Backup data**: Backups may persist for up to 90 days for disaster recovery purposes
- **Legal obligations**: Some data may be retained longer if required by law

## Children's Privacy

Chain Day is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such information.

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
