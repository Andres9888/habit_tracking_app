## 3. Keyword Field (100 chars, comma-separated, no spaces)

```
streak,routine,daily,goals,chain,checklist,reminder,wellness,self-improvement,morning,challenge,journal
```
(99 characters)

**Rationale:** Exclude words already in title/subtitle (habit, tracker, build, streaks, break). Apple indexes title + subtitle separately, so the keyword field should contain *complementary* terms only. No duplicates, no spaces after commas, no app name.

## 4. Category Recommendation

| | Category | Rationale |
|---|----------|-----------|
| **Primary** | Health & Fitness | Highest intent match for habit tracking; less saturated than Productivity for this niche |
| **Secondary** | Productivity | Captures broader "get things done" searches |

## 6. Screenshot Caption Strategy

### Best-Converting Caption Patterns (based on industry data)

| Screenshot # | Caption Text | Visual Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Don't Break the Chain"** | Hero shot — long streak calendar view |
| 2 | **"Track Any Habit in Seconds"** | Adding a habit (simple UI) |
| 3 | **"See Your Progress Grow"** | Stats/charts screen |
| 4 | **"Smart Reminders, Never Forget"** | Notification preview |
| 5 | **"Beautiful. Simple. Effective."** | Dark mode showcase |
| 6 | **"Your Data Stays Private"** | Trust/privacy messaging |

### Caption Guidelines
- **Font size:** 40-60pt, bold, max 6 words per caption
- **Position:** Top 1/3 of screenshot (visible without scrolling)
- **Color:** White text on dark overlay or brand green (#059669) text on light
- **First screenshot is everything:** 70%+ of users never scroll — lead with the strongest emotional hook
- **Social proof:** If/when available, add "★★★★★ Loved by X users" to screenshot 1 or 6
- **Localization:** Translate captions for top 5 markets (EN, ES, DE, JA, PT-BR)

## 7. Additional ASO Recommendations

### Ratings & Reviews
- Prompt for review after 7-day streak (user is engaged + happy)
- Never prompt on a broken streak day
- Use `StoreKit.requestReview()` — max 3x/year per Apple guidelines

### A/B Testing Priority
1. Icon variants (green chain vs calendar vs checkmark)
2. Screenshot order (stats-first vs streak-first)
3. Subtitle variants ("Build Streaks" vs "Daily Routine Builder")

### Seasonal Keyword Opportunities
- **January:** "new year resolution,new year habits,2026 goals"
- **September:** "back to school routine,fall routine"
- **Monday spikes:** Habit app downloads peak on Mondays — time any feature launches accordingly

### Localization Priority (by market size)
1. 🇺🇸 English (US) — base
2. 🇪🇸 Spanish — high growth market
3. 🇩🇪 German — strong App Store spend
4. 🇯🇵 Japanese — high ARPU
5. 🇧🇷 Portuguese (BR) — large underserved market
