# App Store Review Response Templates

Playbook for responding to Chain Day App Store and Google Play reviews. Respond
to **every** review — studies show that responded reviews improve conversion and
average rating by reducing unanswered complaints.

> **Response time target:** < 24 hours for 1-2 star, < 72 hours for 3-5 star.

---

## ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Five-Star Reviews

### Generic positive
> Thank you so much for the kind words! 🙌 Building streaks with you is exactly
> what we had in mind when we made Chain Day. Keep up the incredible work —
> your habits are shaping your future one day at a time!

### Mentions a specific feature (streaks, heatmap, etc.)
> We're thrilled that [FEATURE] is working so well for you! 🎉 It's one of our
> favourite features too. If you ever have ideas for making it even better, hit
> us at support@chainday.app — we read everything.

### Long-time user / milestone mention
> [NAME / first name if available], this made our day! Celebrating your
> [X-day / X-week / X-month] milestone with you is exactly why we built Chain
> Day. Thank you for trusting us with your habits. 💚

## ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Four-Star Reviews

### General praise, mild suggestion
> Thanks for the 4 stars and the honest feedback! We're always improving Chain
> Day, and comments like yours help us prioritise what matters most. If you'd
> like to share more details, drop us a line at support@chainday.app — we'd
> love to hear more about [THEIR SUGGESTION].

### "Would be 5 stars if…"
> We appreciate the honest review! The [FEATURE THEY MENTIONED] improvement is
> something we're actively thinking about. Keep an eye on updates — we ship
> frequently and your feedback directly shapes our roadmap. 🚀

## ⭐⭐⭐ Three-Star Reviews

### Neutral / disappointed but not angry
> Thank you for taking the time to share your experience. We're sorry Chain Day
> hasn't fully clicked for you yet. We'd love to understand what's getting in
> the way — reach us anytime at support@chainday.app and we'll do our best to
> turn this around for you.

### Missing feature
> We hear you! [FEATURE] is on our radar and we understand why it matters.
> We can't share a timeline right now, but we're actively working on making
> Chain Day the best habit tracker out there. Your feedback goes straight to
> the product team. Thanks for sticking with us! 💪

## ⭐⭐ Two-Star Reviews

### Bug / technical issue
> We're really sorry you've run into this — that's not the experience we want
> for you at all. Our team is on it. Could you email us at support@chainday.app
> with your device model and iOS version? That helps us reproduce and fix it
> fast. We'll follow up directly and want to make this right.

### Disappointed with a change / removed feature
> Thank you for the honest feedback — this helps us more than you know. We made
> [CHANGE] because [BRIEF REASON], but we hear you that it's not landing well.
> Please reach out at support@chainday.app so we can learn more about your
> workflow and see how we can make Chain Day work better for you.

### Pricing / paywall concern
> We understand that pricing is personal. Chain Day does offer a free tier with
> core habit tracking at no cost. If you hit a specific limit that felt
> surprising, email us at support@chainday.app — we're happy to chat. We want
> to earn every subscription through value, not walls.

## ⭐ One-Star Reviews

### App crash / data loss
> We sincerely apologise — losing habit data or experiencing crashes is
> unacceptable and we take this very seriously. Please email us immediately at
> support@chainday.app with:
> - Your device model and iOS / Android version
> - Approximate time the issue occurred
> - Any steps to reproduce
>
> We want to fix this urgently and restore your trust. Your data and your
> experience matter to us.

### Sync / cloud issue
> We're so sorry your data isn't syncing correctly. This is a top priority for
> us. Please reach out at support@chainday.app with your account email and a
> brief description of what you're seeing — we'll investigate and get you sorted.

### "Doesn't work at all"
> Thank you for letting us know. We want every user to have a smooth experience
> and clearly something went wrong for you. Please email support@chainday.app
> — our team monitors this closely and will respond quickly to diagnose and fix
> your issue.

### Angry / frustrated tone (general)
> We're genuinely sorry Chain Day let you down. Feedback like this, even when
> it stings, makes us better. If you're open to it, please reach us at
> support@chainday.app — we'd like to understand exactly what happened and
> do whatever we can to make it right.

### Competitor comparison
> Thank you for the candid feedback. We know [COMPETITOR] has been around
> longer, and we respect that. Chain Day takes a different approach — we focus
> on [OUR DIFFERENTIATOR] — and we're building fast. If you give us another
> chance, we'd love to win you over. Reach us at support@chainday.app anytime.

## 📋 Response Best Practices

### Do
- **Personalise** — use the reviewer's name (or "friend") and reference their
  specific feedback
- **Acknowledge** before explaining — show you heard them
- **Invite off-store conversation** — always include `support@chainday.app`
- **Keep it concise** — 2-4 sentences is ideal; reviewers don't read walls
- **Stay positive** — even with angry reviews, respond with empathy
- **Thank them** — every reviewer took time to write

### Don't
- Don't argue or be defensive
- Don't offer refunds publicly (handle privately)
- Don't use copy-paste responses verbatim — personalise every time
- Don't ignore reviews, even old ones
- Don't promise specific release dates you can't guarantee
