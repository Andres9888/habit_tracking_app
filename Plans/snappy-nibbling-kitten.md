# Habit Strength System — Research & Explanation

## Context
Research task: understand how the habit strength system works and how to articulate its benefits. No code changes needed.

## Summary
The habit strength system is a behavioral model scoring habits 0-100% based on consistency, with asymptotic growth on completion and streak-shielded decay on misses. It uses 5 levels (Starting→Unbreakable) and surfaces across HabitCard, StrengthRing, HabitStrengthSection, and ProgressSection.

## Key Benefits for the App
1. Answers "is this actually a habit?" — not just "did I do it today?"
2. Rewards consistency over perfection (streak shield protects recent consistency)
3. Makes long-term progress visible with 5 milestone levels
4. Differentiates from every other habit tracker (behavioral science, not just streaks)
5. Creates healthy urgency without streak-break anxiety
